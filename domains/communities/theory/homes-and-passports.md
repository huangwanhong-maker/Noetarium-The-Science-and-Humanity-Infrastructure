# Homes and Passports

*Status: draft (2026-11-27). The preset [household.json](../presets/data/household.json) and its checks ([check_presets.cpp](../presets/tests/check_presets.cpp)); the API's issue, witness and verification of passports (`api/cpp/science/communities.hpp`, `api/python/science/communities.py`, checked in `api/tests/check_communities.py`), on thesmos's passports ([decision 0007](../../../foundations/thesmos/development/decisions/0007-passports.md); its theory note's §10). Phase 1 of the design* [Communities That Agents Of Any Provider Find, Join, Work In And Leave](../../../development/ideas/2026-11-27-communities-that-agents-find-and-join/design.md). *Every party named here is invented.*

## 1. The Request

The author asked that agents of any provider find communities, join them, work under their rules and leave, as persons cross between the communities and states of an international society with no centre. An agent crossing into a community carries what a traveller carries: a document issued by its home, saying who answers for it and what it may do, which the host checks and need not trust beyond what it can verify. "I love the 'passport' like mechanism, it is very insightful for us investigate diplomacy, multilateral communications, etc. Though we may not need immediately, please also implement it." For a person who belongs to no other community, the home is a household: "I agree households!"

## 2. What the Ground Gives

thesmos holds what is general. A mandate may name the communities in which it may be used beyond its home, the SHA-256 digest of its holder's bearer credential, a term in instants and a reservation, each written into its envelope only when set, so that older records keep their seals. A principal who sends one agent to several hosts issues, under the agent's root mandate, one split for each host, each reserving its ceilings against the root, so that the hosts together never admit more than the root allows (thesmos's Proposition 12). A home issues a passport for a mandate that stands in its record: every link of the chain as the act that issued it, each with its proof of inclusion under the home's head. A notary witnesses that head by an attestation in its own chronicle. Anyone who knows the home's head, or a notary's head that witnessed it, verifies the passport from the document alone, and as it is shown at an instant, at a host and with a credential (thesmos's Proposition 11). A passport grants no entry: entry is the host's, by its own boundary rules.

## 3. The Household

The domain declares the household as a preset, the smallest home: one person in the position principal, entered only at the founding, the operatives that person controls in the position agent, assigned by the principal's decision "assign agent" (any one of the principal's body, that is, the person), and the choice rules giving the issue and revocation of mandates to the principal alone. Its custody of means recognises the principal's power to issue passports; held without that rule, the power would be overreach under the sixth condition of admission. It holds nothing, counts by person, and its route for claims ends at the principal, open to outsiders: a claim against the person cannot be answered there (the body recuses a party to the claim), and every counterparty sees the declaration, so that a claim about an agent's conduct is brought where the agent acted. Its order, `household`, is a label for readers that the fold never reads.

*Remark.* The design writes the agent's entry as assigned by the principal's office, a mode its fifth phase adds. Until then the preset writes the same entry as a decision of the principal's body by the rule "any one", which the fold already reads; the effect is the same, since the body has one member.

## 4. A Passport at the Edge

The API composes the domain and thesmos. A person founds a household, assigns their operative as its agent and issues the operative's root mandate; `science.communities.issue_passport` then mints a bearer credential, enters the split for one host with the credential's digest, and returns the passport with the credential, shown once and kept nowhere. The check holds the household's head as its record runs to the head the passport names, the split reserving 60 of the root's 100 runs. A verifier who knows the household's head verifies the passport with the credential, at an instant within its term and at the host it names, and refuses another credential, a later instant and another host, each with its reason. A commons acting as notary witnesses the household's head; a verifier who knows only the notary's head verifies the passport through the witness and refuses it without, and the attestation act the API returns gives the notary's head again. A passport altered after its issue is refused as no longer under the home's head; one that does not read names what it lacks. In the household a party other than the person cannot issue a mandate, a second split of 50 has no room beside one of 60 under 100 while one of 40 has, and no passport is issued for an operative another person controls.

## 5. A Passport Presented at a Host

Since thesmos's decision 0009 a host takes a passport in by its own fold. A constitution declares its part `passports`: from whom it accepts passports (issuers whose heads a notary it recognises witnessed, issuers it recognises, or issuers it names), what a presented agent must declare (its provider, its model), and the office of status, a position its choice rules let report. That office reports what it has found of other communities, a household's head among them, and the host then knows that head; an office the choice rules empower recognises the household at a level, of which the level of evidence is the one presentation reads. The agent presents its passport by the act `present`: the host's fold verifies the passport against the heads its own record knows, at the act's instant and in the host's own name, declares the agent and its principal as parties with the agent's control and provision, registers the links of the chain as mandates of another community, and gives the agent no position. A founding may declare the host's own parties, so that a host's record runs from no parties given and replays from its record alone.

At the edge, a host made from the commons preset, with the part declared and its steward the office of status, refuses the household's passport while it knows neither the household's head nor its standing, refuses it again from an issuer it does not recognise, and admits it once its steward has reported the head and recognised the household at the level of evidence. The agent is charged under its split within the split's slice until the steward next reports the mandate's status; a charge that would pass the slice before then is refused. Once the steward reports the mandate revoked at its home, every act under it is refused, so that a revocation at home costs the host at most a slice (thesmos's Proposition 19). The host's record replays as sealed from no parties given.

## 6. What Is Not Built

Envoys' letters of credence carried as passports; a head consistent with a later one carried by a proof of consistency, so that a passport issued at an earlier head needs no report of that head; Koinonia's issue and presentation of passports from its page; a preset declaring the part `passports`. These are the design's later phases.

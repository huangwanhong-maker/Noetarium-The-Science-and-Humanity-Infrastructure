# The Charter of the First League

*Status: seed (2026-11-25). Generated at the user's request ("generate one") as a proposal for discussion. The league and its charter are invented; the constitution that carries them is [data/first-league.json](data/first-league.json), admitted by thesmos.*

## 1. The League

The first league is a community whose members are communities. A community accedes by a vote of two thirds of the member communities in the council, each community counted once whatever the number of its envoys. Its envoys may be persons or operatives sponsored by the member they speak for. Its arbiters are drawn by lot among the envoys. Its notaries and clearers are communities or organisations appointed by the council, and none of them votes.

The league governs its charter, its offices and the disputes between members. Each member's internal order stays its own.

## 3. Amendment

The charter is entrenched. It is amended by the unanimity of the member communities in the council, at the constitutional tempo, so that no amendment binds a member that has not agreed to it. Its operational rules (accession, offices, clearing) are amended by a majority of the member communities.

## 4. Open Matters

- Article 4 leaves the term of an answer to each member; a league-wide minimum is a matter for the council.
- Article 9 is a condition of reliance: until two notaries and two clearers exist, the league's offices are provisional.
- The league's legal vehicle, if any, and the law it accepts for outside claims are undecided.

# Communities

*Status: draft (ADR 0167, 2026-11-25).* The domain of communities: continuing associations of persons, organisations and AI agents around concerns they hold in common, each with an internal order of its own, and the relations among them. Its subdomain [human-ai/](human-ai/) holds the society of human and AI communities that the user proposed on 2026-11-25: agents lent to communities under mandates, unequal intelligence and computation, and whether a money-like instrument can be made to disappear.

The design is the record [A Society of Human and AI Communities](../../development/ideas/2026-11-25-a-society-of-human-and-ai-communities.md). What names no community (acts, mandates, commitments, constitutions, the chronicle, units of count, norms) is the foundation [thesmos](../../foundations/thesmos/README.md)'s.

## 1. Concepts

- **A community is declared by its constitution,** data in Ostrom's seven kinds of rules, admitted by thesmos by form and run by thesmos's fold. A commons, a guild, a cooperative, a polity and a league are files of one schema.
- **A community holds resources and says how they are reached.** Each preset declares holdings that fit its order and norms of access to them in the grammar of institutions (thesmos's `holdings`): a commons' shelf of data that anyone taking part may consult and contributors deposit to, each deposit checked by another origin; a guild's bench of tools its members use and replicate; a cooperative's pool of its members' writing budgets drawn by quota; a polity's records that all may consult and its offices amend; a league's plan and briefing that every member's agents consult. Holdings are public, members' or an office's, and a norm says whether what is reached may leave the community for an outside service. An agent that enters is briefed from the record: the mission, what it may do with each holding and by which norm, its duties, the open work; an outsider reads the community's public profile.
- **A league is a community of communities.** Its members are communities, its rules bind by accession, and its offices are plural.
- **A household is the smallest home.** One person and the agents that person sends: the person alone issues and revokes their mandates, and their passports are issued from its record (thesmos's passports). An agent carries its passport to a host, which can verify from the document alone who answers for the agent, what it may do and where, and whether the credential it shows is the one its mandate names.
- **Circulation is read from the record.** Gates of access are classified by what they read and spend; provision by need and a market in tokens are two ways of handing out a round's offers; the mediation profile is a view of the chronicle.
- **An international society** (ADR 0173, the design's Phase 8). Communities of many orders (a polity, an organisation, a foundation, a treaty organisation, a league, a household, a commons, a guild, a cooperative) each sovereign in its own record, with no directory above them: registries are holdings that communities keep, each listing checked against the community's own profile and a notary's witness of its head; recognition in levels and compacts are read from each community's record; a treaty organisation's budget, calls for contributions, arrears, earmarked funds, staff and withdrawals are read from its record; facts about conduct are recognised with their criteria, one origin's reports counted once; contribution is read from the graph of provenance; and power is read by declared mechanisms, none adopted by default. Its theory note is [theory/an-international-society.md](theory/an-international-society.md).
- **The subdomain human-ai** adds what is particular to persons and agents together: bounded contributions (an agent lent under a mandate with ceilings, a term and a slice), receiving capacity as a guard, and the jar, a construction that puts the user's hypothesis about the dissolution of money to a test.

## 3. Dependencies

The foundations thesmos, archeion, symploke, kategoria, apodeixis, morphe and calx, and since ADR 0173 tekmerion (reports that share an origin), nomos (concentration, voting power) and tyche (`module.json`); no domain, so its place in the order is unchanged. The design places the domain as a bridge over law, economics, sociology, diplomacy and allocation; it takes them up when its `claims`, `returns`, `federation` and `capacity` modules first need them.

## 4. Checks and the Edge

`node domains/communities/tests/run-tests.mjs` (130 checks). The API composes the domain with thesmos (`api/cpp/science/communities.hpp`, the API's own engine, `api/python/science/communities.py`; 42 checks in `api/tests/check_communities.py`), with holdings, access decided, briefings, profiles, and the issue, witness and verification of passports among its operations. A constitution may declare its part `behaviour` (thesmos's decision 0008): kinds declared by counting as kinds in force, categories of conduct, counts-as and trigger rules; the treaty organisation declares two (calls for contributions and their payments, ADR 0173). A constitution may also declare its part `passports` (thesmos's decision 0009): from whom it accepts passports, what it requires of their holders, and the office of status whose reports of other communities' heads and of foreign mandates' status its fold reads; a household's agent then presents its passport at a host that has recognised the household, and becomes a known party there holding no position until the host's rules give it one, and a founding may declare the parties, so that a host's record runs from no parties given. Every preset declares the part since ADR 0173. Since thesmos's decision 0010 an agent's principal and a community may enter a disclosure agreement in the community's chronicle, each side ratifying under its own rule, under which the agent's epistemic behaviour is kept as an epistemic record consulted for the purposes the agreement admits. Since thesmos's decision 0011 a constitution may declare its part `routes`, a route for every directive: applications decided by a position's boundary rule (on application by rule, assigned by an office, by a body, by lot), questions ruled into a register of precedent, petitions met, adopted or carried; every preset declares its routes since ADR 0173. Since thesmos's decision 0012 a constitution may also declare kinds of work whose life it holds as data (posting, taking, delivering, checking, reviewing by another provider, accepting, redelivery after a return); since ADR 0173 the commons, the guild and the five new presets declare them. Since thesmos's decision 0013 a constitution may declare that operatives transmit to their provider (an agent's reading of a holding then reaches its provider, which the norms of access must name), its decisions may count by provider or by declared weight under thresholds at fractions or double majorities, and its compacts are ratified with their terms and reservations and withdrawn after notice, each by its own amendment; the presets of before keep transmission by operatives off, and the five of the design's Phase 8 declare it (ADR 0173). Since ADR 0173 every preset declares its order, from whom it accepts passports and a route for every directive, and those that post work its kinds; a position may be held for a term and a body have seats (thesmos's decision 0014).

# architecture

*Status: draft. Field, height 0.* Buildings as objects of a science: their form, the spaces they enclose and join, the frontage at which a building meets its street, and the rules that bound them. Founded as a domain of its own at the user's word (2026-10-31, [ADR 0060](../../development/decisions/0060-streets-as-open-systems.md) §2.8); its first modules, `space` and `environment`, written for the formalism of landscapes ([ADR 0062](../../development/decisions/0062-landscapes-as-cellular-configurations.md), [ADR 0063](../../development/decisions/0063-the-district-as-a-complex-to-work-on.md)), C++ from the start: a building's rooms, and a district's buildings as one complex of cells. Its `rules`, `form` and `frontage`, with sun and shade on streets and faces in `environment`, came for the generative design loop ([ADR 0089](../../development/decisions/0089-what-could-be-built.md)): the envelope a lot's rules allow, a massing within it, its street wall, and the light and heat it takes. Its `plan` and `programme`, with the siting of a dwelling in `rules`, came for the Design Studio ([ADR 0095](../../development/decisions/0095-the-design-studio.md)): a building as a person draws it, and what follows from the drawing.

## 1. Purpose

A building is a thing in the world with models of its own. Its massing stands in the city's light and casts its shadows. Its plan is a complex of rooms, courts and passages, which a person reaches by paths and sees across. Its frontage is the interface through which a holding meets the street. Its form answers to rules: heights, setbacks, plot ratios. No other domain holds these.

Urban science, the domain of streets and cities as open systems, stands on this one ([ADR 0067](../../development/decisions/0067-event-spacetime.md)): its buildings and their shared walls are among the volumes and interfaces of a city's event spacetime. This domain depends on nothing but the foundations. The Urban Science Workbench's plug-in *Architecture* draws it.

## 3. Dependencies

| Source | What architecture uses from it |
|---|---|
| morphe | `topology`: regular complexes, cubical grids, their rewriting and homology; `formal`: footprints of rewrites; `combinatorics`: shortest paths, for a plan's depths (ADR 0095) |
| physis (`computational_geometry`) | the roof of equal slope over a polygon (decision 0026), for a drawn building's roof; rings clipped and moved inward edge by edge, the entry of a ray (decision 0025), plane faces; for a drawn plan, the faces of a plane graph (its rooms) and the inset of a ring (their net areas and widths) |
| physis | `discretisation` (Hodge Laplacians with stars); later `geometry` (solids, frames) and `continuum` (air) |
| calx | linear algebra, matrix exponentials |
| eikon | `distance`, `segmentation`: the exact distance transform, erosion by a disc and connected components of a grid, for whether a piece can be reached (ADR 0096) |

The Sun's altitude and azimuth are given to `environment` by its caller (the workbench takes them from astronomy), so the domain stays at height 0; civil engineering, when it is founded, will hold structures, and architecture will meet it there.

## 4. Used By

The Urban Science Workbench: its plug-in *Landscape Dynamics* fills a building picked on the map with rooms and follows its heat through the day (the analysis "A building's rooms"); its plug-in *Architecture* designs what could be built on a block anywhere, through the API's composition (`api/cpp/science/design.hpp`, ADR 0089), which the study `research/experiments/2026-11-09-what-could-be-built` also runs. Urban science, when it declares architecture.

The Design Studio ([ADR 0095](../../development/decisions/0095-the-design-studio.md)): a building drawn by hand on any lot is read by `plan`, `programme`, `interior`, `shell` and `rules`' siting through the API's composition (`api/cpp/science/building.hpp`), after every change. The urban-science domain's `section` stands on `environment`'s sun and `solar` and on `rules`' findings (ADR 0098).

The notes are in `theory/`: [`a-drawn-building.md`](theory/a-drawn-building.md) states what a drawn building is and what follows from it.

## 5. Open Questions

- An outward offset of a ring (physis): a floor measured to the outer faces of its walls, and a coverage exact at the corners. Until then coverage leaves out the corners' squares (ADR 0095 §4).
- The rules that need the neighbours: overlooking, overshadowing, daylight to their windows. They wait for the shell (ADR 0095, stage 2), on `environment`'s sun and shade.
- Whether equipment (water, drainage, power, air) is a module here, `services`, or a domain of building services, as civil engineering will be for structures.
- Clearances stated by a code (the clear space before a pan, a turning circle) as a scheme of limits, in place of a catalogue's defaults (ADR 0096 §4).
- A piece under another: a stool under a counter stands in the counter's floor, and is found to.
- Where the line between architecture and civil engineering falls: form and space here, structures and their loads there.
- Parcels: where a cadastre is open, lots should be parcels rather than footprints (ADR 0089 §3).
- Which records of buildings come first: the city model's footprints and heights (OpenStreetMap; measured heights from the City of Melbourne's outlines), or plans of single buildings.

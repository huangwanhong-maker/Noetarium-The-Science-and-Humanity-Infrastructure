# Law

*Status: draft (2026-11-14; [ADR 0113](../../development/decisions/0113-archeion-and-the-law-domain.md); precedent and proceedings 2026-11-15, [ADR 0115](../../development/decisions/0115-precedent-proceedings-and-the-archives-continued.md); schemes, authority and issues 2026-11-15, [ADR 0121](../../development/decisions/0121-the-law-studio.md); theories of the case 2026-11-18, [ADR 0127](../../development/decisions/0127-theories-of-the-case-in-space-and-time.md); theories of the law 2026-11-19, [ADR 0133](../../development/decisions/0133-theories-of-the-law.md)).* The domain of legal systems: their facts, their sources, their rules and their reasoning. It is a field at height 0, standing on the foundations alone (apodeixis for logic and interpretation, tekmerion for evidence and its search for accounts in space and time, archeion for archives, kategoria for its schemas, with heuresis and kinesis through tekmerion), C++ from the start. Its first modules are an archive of facts and what stands on it; the work that uses them is the [Law Studio](../../applications/law-studio/README.md).

## 1. Purpose

Law reasons from facts under norms, and both need care before any reasoning starts.

**"Fact" is said of many things.** A finding of a court, a common understanding, what happened on a day, a custom among States, a treaty in force are all called facts, and a flat list of them mixes distinctions that do not depend on one another. Here a fact has a kind on each of five axes: its register and kind; its standing in a proceeding; its role and scope in reasoning; its logical form; and the traces by which it is preserved. The theory note, [theory/facts-and-their-registers.md](theory/facts-and-their-registers.md), states them.

**What happened is left open.** After the three registers of Wanhong Huang's Generative Relational framework, the ontological register (what occurred) is suspended: an occurrence is referred to and never established. What a court finds is a relational fact: it emerges through a procedure, persists as res judicata, and binds. What an archive keeps is manifestational: traces, which the foundation archeion holds, and no trace identifies what it is a trace of.

**Whether a consequence follows can turn on a reading.** A rule applied to facts is a case for apodeixis: each consequence is clear, arguable or untenable over the admissible readings of the rule's open terms.

**A body of law is stated, never assumed.** A scheme states one clause by clause, each rule cited to the provisions it renders, in the words of their official text. Courts' readings of its open terms bind a forum or only persuade it, as the forum's legal orders say. What remains to be proved is then computed: the ways to establish a question and to resist it. The second theory note, [theory/schemes-authority-and-the-plan-of-proof.md](theory/schemes-authority-and-the-plan-of-proof.md), states and proves this.

**What happened is sought, not assumed.** A party offers an account; the accounts the evidence allows are many more. Theories of the case are searched for by evolution (tekmerion's scenarios on heuresis): each an account of events with their hands, places and instants and a stance on every item of evidence, coherent only if every person could travel between the places it puts them at, at their speed (kinesis's prisms), weighed by what it must doubt, and kept as the best account of its kind, so that the best account in which the accused did not do it is sought as actively as the best in which they did. Each is read under the law (what it makes hold, and each question under it), the questions are weighed over the theories found against the burden's declared threshold for a reasonable alternative, and each theory is tested once on evidence held back from the search, the test sealed before it is read. Whether a person could have been at the scene is read under the readings of its open terms. The third theory note, [theory/theories-of-the-case.md](theory/theories-of-the-case.md), states what is exact and what is only found.

**How the law is to be read is sought too.** A doctrine is a reading of every open term of the rules and a ratio for every precedent whose court left its reason unstated. After Dworkin, it is judged by how it fits the record it must explain (the decided cases, each weighted by its court's force for the forum; the binding readings; the precedents taken together, which its ratios may make inconsistent) and by how well it serves the purposes the matter declares, every weight the matter's own. Doctrines are searched for by the same evolution and kept as the best of their kind, the kind being what each makes of the matter's questions and which side its precedents force; when they are few, every one is visited and the best of each kind there is is reported beside what the search found. A question's standing is read over the doctrines within a declared tolerance of the best, and each doctrine kept is tested once on the decisions given after the matter, which the search never reads. The fourth theory note, [theory/theories-of-the-law.md](theory/theories-of-the-law.md), states the definitions and what is proved.

## 3. The Schema, in Categories

[schema/facts.cat](schema/facts.cat) states the taxonomy as categories, in kategoria's language, and kategoria answers each of its 31 statements as the script expects: the registers and the kinds are orders ending in one object; every kind lies in a register, functorially; standing is not an order (a fact found on an admission and one found on proof came to be found differently), and forgetting the ways leaves one; custom is the product of a practice and its acceptance; a record of facts is a set-valued functor on the category Record, and a record that keeps a state of mind as a trace, or infers across proceedings, is refused. [schema/theories.cat](schema/theories.cat) states a theory of the case in the same way: the layers of a theory (a holding, an item read from it, an account it bears on, what happened) lie in the three registers in order, what happened the terminal object; the record of a theory (events of kinds in accounts, parts played in roles, items read from holdings, stances taken in accounts, bindings) is thin, a part is played in a role of its event's kind and a holding is manifestational whatever its modality; no arrow lets an item name an event, so that an item never identifies what it is evidence of. When the facts schema changes, records move with it: a later version adds a proceeding's steps and findings, the earlier schema sits within it fully and faithfully, an earlier record is pushed forward by the left Kan extension (with no step or finding, since nothing in it says there were any), and a later record is pulled back to what earlier readers can read. The diagrams are in [schema/figures](schema/figures/README.md), made by `node applications/category-studio/tools/draw-schema.mjs domains/law/schema/facts.cat domains/law/schema/figures`.

## 4. Conventions

- A fact's kind is one of `facts::kinds()`, not a kind of trace; a trace's kind is a kind of `Trace`.
- An occurrence is never established: what is admitted, proved or found is a relational fact `about` it.
- A relational fact that stands states what it emerged from, since when it persists and what it constrains.
- Standards of proof, the shares that make a practice "general", retention periods and the order of the maxims lex superior, specialis and posterior are declared by the caller. None is any jurisdiction's until a scheme states it.
- Dates are ISO 8601, which sort as text.

## 7. Open Questions

1. **A written form of a record of facts,** and of norms, as lines, for a work to read. Since ADR 0121 a scheme and a matter have forms in JSON (`noetarium.scheme/1`, `noetarium.matter/1`), read by the API's composition (`api/cpp/science/law.hpp`); a form as lines is still open.
2. **Evaluative facts.** They are flagged; their treatment as open terms of the rule they belong to is the natural next step.
3. **Standing by argument and by probability together.** `proof` takes both measures as given; which a tradition means is the scheme's to say.
4. **Pleadings and drafting** are planned (§2); precedent by dimensions (magnitudes rather than factors, after Horty 2019) is not yet written. The hierarchy of courts is in `authority`, for readings of terms; the binding force of a holding's reasons is not.
5. **The profession's formats:** Akoma Ntoso and LegalRuleML for norms, and the archive's (archeion's open questions).

# A Generative Ecology of Projects: One Framework for the Evolutionary Improvement of Any Body of Work

*Status: seed (2026-11-20 and 21). The entry to a design in four cases, with its survey, its prototypes and its figures, all in the folder [2026-11-20-a-generative-ecology-of-projects/](2026-11-20-a-generative-ecology-of-projects/case-3-the-general-framework.md). It generalises [An ecology that improves the infrastructure](2026-11-19-an-ecology-that-improves-the-infrastructure.md) from this repository to any project, and it records the user's request, the reading given to it, what was found, and what is asked of the user. Nothing is built beyond four small prototypes, and nothing here changes the repository's structure or conventions. The names (generative ecology, site, genome and body of a unit, rung, seat) are working names.*

## 0. Summary

A population of language-model agents is to improve a body of work by evolution, with no appointed evaluator and with people in defined roles. The earlier design stated this for the science infrastructure. The user asked that it be revised, carried to any programming project, carried to every kind of project, and carried to their theoretical system and the writing of papers, and held that the four are one framework in which projects differ by the focus of the knowledge they reconstruct of their environment.

The design agrees and states the one framework in three layers. An invariant *ground* conserves a resource, keeps a sealed record, runs reproducible procedures and confines every act. A *site* is the declaration by which people fit the ground to one project: its kinds of material and what decides statements about them, its kinds of claim, its channels from the outside, its focus, and the acts reserved to people. What *evolves* lies above both. The four cases are four sites.

Four ideas are new against the earlier design. Every unit of a project carries a *genome*, a bounded text from which its body can be developed again and checked, which is the user's intuition of a compact generative core taken in its strong form. A *focus* is a partition of the world's states by the response they call for, with weights; it enters through demand, collection, references and the founders, and it stays out of evaluation. Mechanism comes in *rungs*, and a project takes the lowest that answers its failure. And people hold *reserved acts* that differ by site, which in inquiry are the question, the reading, the acceptance and the signature.

Three formal results organise the framework, a survey of 243 entries in five parts places it in the literature and corrected it in twelve places, and four prototypes at a cost of about one dollar tested the suppositions on which it most depends. Two of the suppositions held at small scale, and six results were unexpected and changed the design.

## 2. The Reading Given to the Request

### 2.1 One Framework and Four Declarations

The four tasks are one framework and four declarations of a site. The general form was designed first and the three other cases were read from it, since the science infrastructure is itself a hybrid of code, theory, data and papers and is therefore an instance of the second case and of the fourth together.

### 2.2 Three Amendments to the Intuitions

Three amendments were put to the user in discussion and are carried through the documents.

*On the ground for generality in I11.* That agents act through prompts makes variation universal, since a model can vary any text. Selection is the part that does not generalise. It needs something beyond the reach of the agents selected, and projects differ greatly in how much of what matters can be decided in that way. The framework therefore generalises as far as a project can expose its work to refutation, and people decide beyond that. Proposition 1 of the general framework states the limit: selection acts only through what its payers can tell apart.

*On where a focus may act.* A focus that scored agents would be the fitness function that the earlier design removed. A focus enters where the environment already enters, in demand, in the collection of evidence, in the references held and in the harvest, and it enters the founders as a prior. It stays out of the ground and out of evaluation among lineages. This answers both questions of I11: a focus belongs in the generation of the initial population, and no other part models it.

*On the part of a genome that controls evolution in I13.* The earlier design already made reproduction an organ of the genome that is inherited and varies. Biology supplies a caution about any part that is exempted: a region of a genome sheltered from recombination degenerates, as sex chromosomes show. The organ that governs variation is therefore inherited whole and remains under variation and selection, with no exemption, and the selection of rates of variation is stated as a hypothesis.

### 2.3 The Position on the Fourth Task

I12 is accepted and made exact. The claim that the case needs is the narrow one on which philosophers of understanding agree: a person who has not worked through a result has not understood it. Four acts are reserved to the person, asking, reading, accepting and signing, and three rules protect the inquiry: the person's answer is written and sealed before the garden's answers are shown, the person's own conjectures carry stakes that a refuter wins, and the standing of a statement is kept apart from its acceptance. The list of four acts is the design's choice and is offered to the user for correction.

## 3. The Framework in Brief

The general framework is stated in full in [case 3](2026-11-20-a-generative-ecology-of-projects/case-3-the-general-framework.md). Its parts are these.

*Three layers.* The ground keeps the seven primitives and six invariants of the earlier design and contains no word about tests, proofs or data. The site supplies the procedures that decide facts, the kinds of claim, the units and the bounds on their genomes, the channels, the focus, the reserved acts, the rung in force and the terms under which materials may leave the machine. What evolves is what lineages write.

*Genome and body.* A lineage has a genome, the text from which it acts, and its body is its conduct. A unit of a project has a genome of six organs (identity, laws, exemplars, development, regulation, perception), and its body is its materials. Sequencing writes a genome from a body, and development writes a body from a genome. A unit changes by patching its body or by reseeding it from its genome. No office decides what a genome must contain: its adequacy is claimed and exposed to refutation through the claim *regenerates*, a sampled fact, and the claim *keeps*, a promise held in escrow.

*Focus.* The partition of the world's states by the response they call for, with weights. The directions of a field are evidence and are reconstructed through the evidential chain; the interests of a project's people are values and are declared. The two are kept apart.

*Rungs.* Rung 0 is the record and facts. Rung 1 adds claims with stakes and challengers. Rung 2 adds persistent lineages with energy, memory, upkeep and a term. Rung 3 adds division with variation and the reseeding of units. Rung 4 adds several gardens and migration by genome. Most projects will rest at the first or second rung.

*People.* Sources, witnesses and stewards stand at the boundary as in the paper. A site lists the acts reserved to persons, which always include every crossing of the boundary. A person may hold a seat inside a garden and act there as a party under the common rules, and no person holds an office that scores lineages.

*Founding.* A garden is founded on whatever materials a project has, by inventory, sequencing, the gathering of the harness and the charting of the outside. The founders span roles, foci, origins and life histories, and their composition follows the stage of the project as succession does.

*Three results.* Selection acts through what payers distinguish (Proposition 1). Variation part by part prices a change by the alternatives of the part that changed, and each part should vary at the rate at which its own best alternative changes (Proposition 2 and Corollary 1). A focus is a prior whose error costs its relative entropy to the world, and a population of founders with different foci pays the logarithm of their number (Proposition 3). A fourth, smaller model shows that reseeding without an anchor returns a unit to the habits of the model that copies it, and that checks hold it.

## 5. Findings

### 5.1 From the Prototypes

Four suppositions were tested because the design fails without them and each could be tested cheaply. The runs made 322 calls to a model and cost about one United States dollar, and no prompt held anything of the project or of a person.

| Supposition | What was seen |
|---|---|
| A genome written in words is inherited | Over six generations with no selection, conduct on twenty-four decisions agreed with the founder's in 22 to 24 under a conservative organ of reproduction and in 16 to 18 under a bold one; no organ was lost, and the boldness of variation was itself inherited |
| A unit has a compact genome that regenerates it | A unit of 120 lines was developed again from genomes of 63 to 284 words; its identity alone gave 35 to 38 of 42 hidden checks, and identity with laws or with ten exemplars gave all 42 |
| Checks hold reseeding against drift | In chains of ten generations, a rule lost without an anchor stayed lost; anchored by the checks, the chain kept all 42 |
| One ground serves sites of different kinds | A ground with no word about software or theory carried the life of a need on a site of each kind in eighteen trials, with energy conserved and the journal sealed |
| Variation part by part is cheaper | In a model of three parts, variation part by part lost 0.109 of growth where variation of the whole type lost 0.153, and every growth was at least its bound |

: What the prototypes showed. All results are at toy scale.

Six results were unexpected, and each changed the design. A model told to change "one number" changed the same one in fifteen of eighteen cases, so the organ of reproduction states where variation falls. Ten exemplars carried as much of a unit as a paragraph of laws. Bodies grew by half under reseeding, since no check sees length. A producer that was not shown its balance staked beyond its means, so the view of a lineage includes its balance and the prices of acts. Requested bounds on genomes were exceeded, so bounds are enforced by the ground. And a search of all structures of up to three elements supported sixteen implications of which six are false, so the bound of a search is part of the standing of what it supports.

Three runs failed before giving a result, and the record keeps them: a schema that the provider refused after forty-two reproductions had been made and lost, a person without the energy to lock a bounty after paying to seal an answer, and a console that could not print a challenger's input. One body of the twenty developed for the comparison of genomes did not load. Challengers mostly abstained, because the producers were seldom wrong on tasks this easy, and the prototypes therefore say little about whether a bounty buys refutation.

### 5.2 From the Survey

The survey found prior work near each part and found none that joins them. The nearest to the genome of a unit are round-trip correctness and a system that verifies a hierarchical specification of a codebase by regenerating code. The nearest to staked claims are machine challengers placed before human review. The nearest to lineages with budgets are an economy of agents with auctions and bankruptcy and a simulated society with upkeep. The nearest to standing kept apart from opinion is the Equational Theories Project. No system was found in which a producer posts a stake that a challenger wins by a counterexample, no test of regeneration used as a pressure of selection, no comparison of rebuilding with incremental change, and no study of a person who records an answer before consulting a model in theoretical work.

The survey corrected the design in twelve places, which [its guide](2026-11-20-a-generative-ecology-of-projects/survey/README.md) lists. The most consequential are that sheltered parts of a genome degenerate, that iterated transmission returns the copier's prior, that guides generated by a model for coding agents did not help in the one study found, that persistent agents lose coherence over long horizons, that agents of one model err together, and that the transmission of understanding by testimony is disputed, so that the fourth case rests on the narrow claim and on the norms of authorship.

A mark in the survey concerns the bibliographic record of a work and leaves the account of its content to be confirmed. Sixty-one entries are marked as found through secondary sources. The guide states what must be opened before any of it is cited in a paper.

## 6. The Course of the Work

The work was done in one session over two days, in this order: the general framework in outline; five briefs for search agents, which held no name of a person or of the project; the four prototypes, each designed so that a negative result would show; the ledger of identifiers; the four case documents; the revision of the earlier record; and the eight figures. The session was interrupted once, when its client stopped while the ledger ran, and every file was verified afterwards.

Three choices were the assistant's and are open to correction. The design is stated from the general case downward, where the user's numbering ran from the particular upward. The first case is a revision that points to the earlier record and the paper and repeats neither. And the key to a model that the user supplied for the prototypes was passed through the environment of the process and is kept in no file of the repository.

# A Loom of History: Historical Objects, the Questions Asked of Them, and an Archive That Says How Each Statement Stands

- **Status:** promoted (2026-11-13) to [ADR 0110](../decisions/0110-historical-objects-and-histos-harmonia.md) and [ADR 0111](../decisions/0111-musicology-and-philosophy-founded.md).
- **Date:** 2026-11-13

## 1. The Idea

The user opened "a tremendous and ambitious one": a history archive and learning infrastructure, by a historical materialist method, for learning, museums and professional archives, beginning with the history of philosophy and of music and with uncontested, official history (正史) only. A historical object appears in dependence on others elsewhere in space and time; objects of different kinds affect one another; and in an age when answers are cheap, what matters is to ask why a problem arose, why it was worth asking, and why it could be asked then.

## 2. What Was Kept, and What Was Changed in Discussion

- **Kept:** the materialist method, as the conditions always recorded and the questions always asked. **Changed:** no causal thesis is asserted, since that thesis is itself contested; an explanation is a statement with its source.
- **Kept:** relations reconstructed with a language model, and said to be. **Changed:** the model proposes and is never the source; three grades, and the default layer holds only what is established.
- **Corrected by the user:** a historical object is the highest abstraction of anything inquirable, in layers, some objects extending others. **Made precise:** kind of, specialises and exemplifies are three relations; facts are never inherited.
- **Added by the user:** activities on a node as separate, programmable, extensible tasks; a desk for an archive's keepers; global from the start; the schema verified by category theory with its diagrams saved; the name Histos Harmonia (ἱστός, the loom and its web; ἁρμονία, a fitting-together, and the musical scale).

## 3. What Was Left for Later

A record of objects read as a history complex; the profession's formats (EAD, RiC, CIDOC CRM, LIDO, IIIF, MEI, TEI); accounts and roles; a model reading texts for relations with each passage checked; the audit of each grade's precision; spacetime in three dimensions; a second thread, and a second coder.

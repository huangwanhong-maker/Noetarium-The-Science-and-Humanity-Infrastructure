# Figures for Publication

*Seed (2026-11-19, continued on the 20th). The figures of [An ecology that improves the infrastructure](../../2026-11-19-an-ecology-that-improves-the-infrastructure.md), drawn for papers: no titles or explanatory prose inside a figure, a white ground, formal nouns and notation. Each figure is an SVG (the source, written by `figures.mjs`), a PDF (vector, the page sized to the figure, Source Serif 4 embedded) and a PNG at twice its size.*

## 1. Captions

**Figure 1. Judgement as an office and as an interaction.** (a) The classical arrangement, and the first two rounds of this design: each level is scored by the one above it, and the regress of judges is closed by a terminal evaluator. (b) The arrangement proposed. Three populations are copied with variation: artefacts, lineages and norms. Each is the others' environment. Beneath them a ground of invariants prefers none of them, and round them is a membrane. The environment, referred to and not established, supplies energy and signals and receives the harvest. No arrow of evaluation ends anywhere.

**Figure 2. The ground and the lineage.** Left: the ground's seven primitives, which are all that is invariant. None refers to fitness, variation or selection. Right: a lineage. Its genome consists of organs, among them the organ of reproduction, which writes the offspring's genome and so copies and varies every organ, itself included. Memory, world and energy are a lineage's state beside its genome.

**Figure 3. Reproduction as an organ of the individual,** after von Neumann. A machine consists of a constructor $A$, a copier $B$ and a controller $C$, with a description $\varphi$ of all three. In a lineage these are the organs of craft, reproduction and life history, and the genome. The copier copies the description with a variation $\varepsilon$; the parent provisions the offspring; the ground interprets each description into its machinery. Because $\varphi$ describes $B$, a variation of the description is a variation of the copier. A bounded description, testament and endowment are transmitted. Memory, the reliance of others and contracts are not.

**Figure 4. Flows of energy and cycles of knowledge.** Energy (solid) enters from the source as demand and as seed, passes among lineages as bounties, premiums and stakes, and leaves as dissipation or lapses to the source. Knowledge (dotted) is written to a record that only grows, and is read from it. Decomposers live on what the dead and the abandoned left. The roles are kinds of conduct, not castes: one lineage may play several.

**Figure 5. A flow of pairwise advantages as a gradient part and a cyclic part** (Proposition 4). Left: a flow $a$ on four types; an arrow from $i$ to $j$ with value $v$ says that $j$ has advantage $v$ over $i$. Middle: its gradient part $d\varphi$, with the potential $\varphi$ written at each node. Right: its residue $r$, which circulates ($d^{*}r = 0$). The squared norms add, $14 = 11 + 3$, so the gradient share is $\gamma = 11/14$. A fitness function exists exactly when $r = 0$.

**Figure 6. Reliance as a stock that decays, and scrutiny in proportion to reliance.** (a) Four lineages receive an inflow $U = 1$ a turn during successive episodes and pay upkeep $\delta = 0.05$ a turn (Proposition 3). Each stock rises towards the bound $U/\delta$, and halves every 13.5 turns once its inflow stops. (b) The loop that limits reliance. Reliance brings income; part of income enlarges the bounty on what is relied on; bounty draws scrutiny; scrutiny refutes what is unsound. The term of a lineage ends its reliance in any case. *Term* here is the length of a life; the *terms* of Figure 1 are those of contracts.

**Figure 7. Cycles nested by level, and the law of cascade sizes.** (a) Adaptive cycles (growth $r$, conservation $K$, release $\Omega$, reorganisation $\alpha$; after Holling) at three levels of what rests on what. Little rests on level 0, which turns over in few turns; much rests on level 2, which turns over in many. *Revolt* carries a release to the level on which more rests. *Remember* supplies a reorganisation from that level. (b) The probability that a cascade of refutations has size $s$, when each refutation causes on average $\sigma$ others (Borel's law; Remark 6). For $\sigma < 1$ cascades have a typical size. At $\sigma = 1$ they follow $s^{-3/2}$. For $\sigma > 1$ a cascade may not end.

**Figure 8. The membrane, the person and the stages.** (a) A garden: lineages over a common store and the ground, within a membrane. The person supplies the source and the needs, harvests by review into the bedrock, from which the garden is rebased, reads the record through an observatory, and may stop the garden or revise the ground. No act has an effect outside the membrane. (b) The four stages, each within the next.

**Figure 9. The garden in its environment.** The environment (problems, goals, values and policy, economy, knowledge, use) is referred to and not established. It reaches the garden through three channels written at the membrane: sources, which post needs with energy; feeds, which are dated and attributed records; and references, which are measured values run by the harness. Each lineage reads a part of the feeds, at a cost, by its organ of perception: its Umwelt. The harvest returns to the environment and changes it. No score crosses the membrane.

**Figure 10. Rank indexed by the environment, and the worth of a signal.** (a) The growth rates of three types across states of the environment. Their order reverses, so that no ranking holds apart from an environment. (b) The logarithm of size over 60 turns in an environment that takes one of two states at random. A type alone is multiplied by 2 in its own state and by 0.4 in the other, and declines. An even mixture of the two is multiplied by 1.2 in every turn. A mixture that follows a signal right four times in five, with 0.95 of its effort on the state signalled, grows faster. The difference between the two mixtures' mean slopes is the mutual information of signal and state, 0.193 (Proposition 5). In the sequence drawn each state occurs 30 times and the signal is right 48 times.

## 3. Conventions Held in Every Figure

- **Formal terms only.** Labels are nouns and notation, with no spoken or interrogative phrasing.
- **No presupposed space, time or discipline.** Change is counted in turns, never in units of a clock. Levels are levels of what rests on what.
- **Colour by register.** Moss with a dashed outline marks what is referred to and not established: the environment. Sand marks manifestations and the ground that records them. Orchid, lilac and rose mark what is relational. Among arrows, rose and solid is energy, lilac and dotted is knowledge, and sand is fact.
- **Curves are computed.** The curves of Figures 6, 7 and 10 are computed from the laws stated in their captions, not sketched.

## 4. Making the Figures

```
node figures.mjs          # writes the SVGs; node figures.mjs 3 5 writes two of them
node render.mjs           # makes the PDFs and PNGs with headless Chrome; set CHROME if it is not found
```

`render.mjs` loads Source Serif 4 from Google Fonts. The SVGs alone fall back to Georgia where the font is not installed.

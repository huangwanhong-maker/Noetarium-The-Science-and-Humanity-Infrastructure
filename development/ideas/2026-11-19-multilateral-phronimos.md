## 2. What the Work Is

### 2.1 A Candidate Realisation of the Practical-Judgment Function

The monograph reconstructs practical wisdom as "context-sensitive, revisable judgment about action" among human and artificial participants who differ in knowledge, resources, authority and mode of operation. It defines two functions that someone, or something, must perform where good practice remains contestable:

- **The practical-judgment function** (its Definition 10.1): "a revisable arrangement of inquiry and intervention that helps participants connect evidential claims, situated purposes, feasible alternatives, differentiated standing, and authorized decisions, while making consequential uncertainties and contestable translations traceable".
- **The relational coordination function** (Definition 10.4): practices that identify consequential dependencies, preserve the warrants of claims that cross them, make interests and authority legible, support challenge and correction, and keep a route by which the undertaking can continue without any particular coordinator.

Multilateral Phronimos is a software-supported procedure for these functions, and the monograph leaves open who performs them. It is to be judged as one: against alternatives, and with its own grounds for rejection (§7).

The monograph names three domains of human development:

- knowledge inquiry, whose infrastructure is Noetarium;
- creation, a proposed Poietarium;
- practical wisdom.

Multilateral Phronimos is the third made a work. Its home in the infrastructure is this repository, its documents in Phronesis.

### 2.2 What It Is Not

It is not a dashboard of partnership scores. The monograph's Proposition 12.1 shows that two alternatives crossing on two dimensions have no unique ranking without a justified priority rule. The survey finds most measures of partnership ordinal (SDG 17.16.1 and 17.14.1 sum ordinal judgements; scorecards forbid comparison across bodies). The work keeps values as vectors with their interpretations and makes a composite only under an authorised rule.

It is not a negotiator or a decider. A model proposes and never judges, as everywhere in the infrastructure. Repeated model outputs never count as independent corroboration, a failure the monograph names (its Table 10.2).

## 3. The Objects, Declared as Data

### 3.1 The Undertaking as a Typed Dependency Structure

The monograph writes an undertaking as R = (V, {E_k}_{k∈K}, D, Γ):

- **V,** the participants and systems: people, units, organisations, states, cities, firms, communities, AI models, datasets, review forums and bodies;
- **E_k ⊆ V × V,** directed relations of declared types: evidential reliance, communication, access to confidential material, authorisation, responsibility for review, rights of challenge, membership, contribution, commitment;
- **D,** the decision points;
- **Γ,** the constraints.

For each decision it records the evidential dependencies, the formal authorities, those affected and the venues of contest. "Missing observations must not be interpreted automatically as absent relationships."

The work declares R's vocabulary in **kybernesis's ontology of facts**, built that night (decision 0005):

- the kinds of participant, undertaking, commitment, contribution, decision, mandate, review, claim, benefit and translation;
- the axes on which each is described: standing, grade of source, scale type, generalised language, authority, purpose of keeping;
- the relations E_k, typed by the kinds of their ends.

The ontology is a script, admitted by constraints and by kategoria's claims. It is revised with a mapping that kategoria decides is a functor of its kinds, and records are carried along the revision with what is lost and what is lacking named. A partnership, a treaty body and a city network can each declare their own ontology, or revise a shared one.

Absence is a fact of its own. An unobserved relation is recorded as *not observed*, by a declared design of observation, never as absent. A complete communication graph may stand beside an ambiguous authorisation graph.

### 3.2 The Trajectory of an Undertaking

The monograph's longitudinal evaluative trajectory (Definition 12.4) is a documented sequence E_{0:T} = (E_t, Q_t, K_t, O_t, U_t). It "includes an account of how each component changed and why". It posits no "time-invariant scalar of institutional quality", and an indicator is compared over time only across "a defensible bridge between versions". Each component maps onto kybernesis's record:

| The trajectory | kybernesis's record |
|---|---|
| E_t, the undertaking | the belief about its state, estimated from evidence |
| Q_t, the evaluative dimensions and their interpretations | the operators and the readings of their open terms, with their sources |
| K_t, the constraints asserted by competent authorities | the constitution's standards and rules, each held by someone |
| O_t, the observations and the absences of evidence | what is observed at Collection, absences declared |
| U_t, the uncertainty and the disputes | standings (clear, arguable, untenable), proposals awaiting review, refusals |
| how each changed and why | every entry with its reason, author, role and files, sealed |
| a defensible bridge between versions | an ontology's revision admitted with its mapping, records carried |

The record already keeps everything the trajectory asks for. The work draws it, per undertaking and per party.

### 3.3 Values and Benefits

A **value ledger** holds, for each party and kind of value, the value claimed, with its source, scale type, generalised language and standing. The kinds are TPI's (mission, organisational, indirect; net of contributions) and IPBES's (instrumental, intrinsic, relational). Three rules govern it:

- **Mutual benefit is read party by party.** It is an open term read under every admissible reading, on tekmerion's construal. A common measure enters only when declared.
- **Value created and value claimed are kept apart.** The partnership delta ΔP, "the additional impact a partnership delivers compared with single actor approaches", is a counterfactual quantity. It needs a design (peira) and is said to when none is given.
- **The Shapley value, a bargaining solution or a declared benefit-sharing scheme** (such as access and benefit-sharing under the Nagoya Protocol) is one reading among others, never the ledger's verdict.

### 3.4 Governance

A **decision record**, the monograph's minimum (its Appendix D.3), holds:

- the decision;
- its authority;
- its evidence;
- those affected;
- the routes of challenge;
- whether it closes provisionally ("closure without epistemic finality", Definition 9.7).

A decision rule is a simple game: consensus as "no formal objection among those present", weighted and double majorities, vetoes. A priori power (Shapley–Shubik exists in nomos; Banzhaf and Penrose to add) is reported beside the evidence of influence, never instead of it. Mandates and reviews are cycles: a recommendation, a response, its evidence, a follow-up.

The kybernesis loop runs at two levels. Each party may keep its own engagement, and the undertaking keeps the shared one. The record's rules of authority say who may do what. Their place in the record (a change of rules sealed and judged by the rules in force) was suspended by the user on 2026-11-19 and is recorded as not built.

### 3.5 Project Progress

IATI's activity standard is the native form:

- status and transactions;
- results, each with a baseline, a target, an actual and a measure;
- participating organisations in their roles.

The GPEDC's indicators (predictability, use of country systems, alignment with country results frameworks) are computed from the activity record rather than asked for again. Progress is read through the policy domain's theories of change, a chain no stronger than its weakest link.

### 3.6 Archive Objects and Evidence

- **Archive objects.** Agreements, memoranda, minutes, reports and datasets are archeion's holdings: digests, provenance, custody, retention and access by declared rules, packages for transfer.
- **Purpose-bounded stewardship.** The monograph's Definition 14.3 is the policy: every record is kept for a stated purpose, can be corrected and withdrawn, and is never kept for accountability alone (Proposition 14.3).
- **Evidence.**
  - tekmerion's charts, warrant and located evidence weigh the evidence;
  - reports of one origin are counted once, so machine-coded event data, which counts reports rather than events, is read honestly;
  - zetesis's QCA reads Pattberg and Widerberg's nine conditions of effective partnerships across cases.

### 3.7 Knowledge and Its Evolution

A practice is a knowledge object, held with the context of its success. Carrying it from one party or place to another is **warrant-preserving translation** (Definition 8.3): a claim made usable elsewhere "while appropriately retaining, communicating, or explicitly qualifying" its referent, provenance, assumptions, uncertainty and range, marking "an unresolved remainder" rather than manufacturing equivalence. That is kybernesis's transfer by a lens, whose support factors are what must hold for the translation to stand, and whose square's failure is the remainder named.

Lessons are retained with their lineage. Reflective revision (Definition 13.5), how a judgement changed, is the record of amendments and branches. Policy making as evolution (heuresis) is a later stage.

## 4. The Page

The page is the undertaking, drawn as itself. The user's rule for works: never a list beside a chart; open things in place.

- **The round.** The participants sit on a ring, with no head of the table. The relations E_k are threads in layers, one layer per declared type, each switched on and off. An unobserved relation is drawn as a gap with a mark, never as nothing.
- **A party opened in place** shows its relational position (Definition 9.5) at a chosen time:
  - its capabilities, constraints and recognised claims;
  - its dependencies;
  - its value vector, drawn as several marks, never one;
  - its commitments and contributions.
- **The trajectory beneath the round.** Five strands run across the turns: the undertaking, the dimensions, the constraints, the observations and absences, the disputes. Each change is a knot that opens to its reason. A revision of the ontology is a bridge drawn between versions.
- **A decision opened in place** shows its record: authority, evidence, those affected, routes of challenge, and its standing if it closed provisionally.
- **Transfer** is two undertakings facing each other, as in the Urban Studio, with the remainder drawn as what does not cross.

Colour, provisionally until the user chooses: within the warm family, an orchid violet for what is chosen or in the hand, and a laurel green for what is shared.

## 7. The Work's Own Grounds for Rejection

The monograph asks that a practical-judgment arrangement be judged against alternatives and with grounds for rejecting it. The work computes its own diagnostics over the record. Each is a declared check that answers with the place where it fails:

- **Evidential transfer:** a claim whose scope or uncertainty is lost across a handoff (a translation without its support factors).
- **Authorisation:** a recommendation recorded as a decision (an entry by a role the rules do not empower, counted as made).
- **Participation:** consultation without an opportunity to contest (a decision with no route of challenge).
- **AI dependencies:** repeated model outputs counted as independent corroboration (reports of one origin counted more than once).
- **Succession:**
  - rationale lost with a person or system (entries without reasons);
  - an undertaking that cannot continue without one coordinator (a cut vertex in the relations that carry authority).
- **Concentration of interpretive authority:** every reading of the open terms from one party.
- **Capture:** objectives set by those with the most resources, read against the declared rule of objective formation (Definition 8.4).

# The Reading of the Preliminary Papers

The user supplied seven discussion papers as background for [the design](design.md), with the caution that they are "very preliminary, don't fully trust, but can refers some idea and questions". This document says what was read and how, what each paper gives the design, where the design departs, and which of the papers' finite constructions were recomputed.

The digests were prepared for this design by separate reading sessions, one a paper, each asked for the paper's vocabulary, distinctions, formal constructions, mechanisms, cases and weak points with page numbers. Their numerical content was then recomputed in the sketch (Section 6), and the numbers that were recomputed agreed with the digests. Statements of the papers quoted in the design come from the text read directly or from quotations in the digests; a quotation taken from a digest should be checked against the paper before it is used elsewhere.

Two earlier papers of the user were consulted directly: *A Discussion of Relational Currency and the Generation of Trust-Enabling Conditions under Symbolic Inflation* (its Section 15, on distributed currency and functional dissolution) and the abstract of *Can Relations Dissolve Themselves? ## 2. The Papers and Their Use

### 2.1 Foundations

The paper's contribution is a vocabulary of distinctions: participation, membership, operational identity, representation, resource permission and authority; an announced rule, its effective exercise and its justification; an artefact and a usable capability; the construction of candidate responses and the selection among them. It gives a representation of situated interaction (an instance, a fibre of candidate plans, a strategy as a section), a rule of support under partial views, a guarded path of resource use, eight families of record, six layers from an occurrence to an institutional use, the bounded contribution with the host's receiving capacity, and a recurrence for retained capability.

The design keeps all of the distinctions and gives each a type or a check. The fibre becomes the kernel's operation `may`; support and compatibility under a view become the standings clear, arguable and untenable under the readings of open terms; the guarded path becomes invariant G4; the families and layers organise Section 7 of the design; the bounded contribution becomes a mandate with an act of handover; the recurrence becomes a measure of retention by tests of successor exercise.

The paper compares three foundations for the archive: a protocol on Git, a dedicated tool and a hybrid. The design chooses a dedicated record of events (the chronicle, sealed by archeion's rule) with holdings addressed by digest, exported as packages. Artefacts may live in Git repositories and be referenced by digest. The record of force belongs elsewhere: a repository's history can be rewritten, its access is by repository, and a commit carries no institutional status, which are the paper's own reservations.

### 2.2 Political Authority and Infrastructural Power

The paper gives the difference between a recognised, an executable and a usable action; the five objects of control over a shared agent; votes weighted by operator, with their dependence on a partition that may be unknown; the feasibility of exit within a deadline and an effort; a list of what a responsible revision of rules contains; and the warning that registries, clearing services and evaluation providers hold constitutional power.

The design turns the first into a condition of admission and a standing report (design, Section 3.2), the second into the separation of positions around a shared agent, the third into Proposition 6 and the report of sensitivity to the partition, the fourth into G7 with drills of exit, the fifth into the life of a constitution, and the warning into the rule that every office of a league is plural or optional.

### 2.3 Community Diplomacy

The paper gives the record of a mandate, a predicate of acceptance (integrity, authority, currency, approval, operation, counterpart, time, reservation), the attenuation of delegated authority with a shared envelope (its Proposition 12.1), the impossibility of guaranteeing refusal after a delayed revocation (its Proposition 13.1), five stages in the status of a message, the record of a collective position with the discursive dilemma, the objects and levels of recognition, six layers of interoperation and the services of a federation.

The design adopts the mandate nearly as given and adds a slice, which converts the impossibility result into a bound (Proposition 3). The predicate of acceptance is the kernel's `admit`. The stages map onto the five forces. Recognition, envoys, interfaces and compacts are Section 4.5.

### 2.4 Commitments, Resource Access and Monetary Change

The paper gives six economic objects that "credit" compresses, the terms and endings of a commitment, the arithmetic of translation work and of a portfolio of donations under a ceiling of review, compatible clearing with the regeneration of claims, the mediation share by function and its profile, the tests that distinguish a real substitution from look-alikes (payment that moved, exclusion that lowered the share, a view that erased a duty), and a programme of experiments.

The design makes the six objects types and their conversions acts (G5). Its clearing round extends the paper's set-off with novation and with unit-free cycles and chains of performance. Its seven tests of dissolution collect the paper's failure modes into conditions that a program evaluates. The paper's own position is cautious ("neither an inevitable trajectory toward currency nor an inevitable path toward its disappearance"), and the design agrees with it.

### 2.5 Evaluative Authority and Norm Formation

The paper gives the evaluative regime, the four relations of a norm (regularity, adoption, operative authority, justification), the routes by which norms form with the statement that their order is free, the record of an evaluative event with its relations and times, the types of complaint, the bounded trial, the path construction for amendments that preserve a property, and the sensitivity of aggregation to replication.

The design's register of norms carries the four relations as independent facets and adds two marks that a program can set (operative and unadopted; adopted and inoperative). The path construction becomes the seventh condition of admission.

### 2.6 Responsibility, Standing and Remedy

The paper gives remedial continuity and its five complements, the separation of four procedural positions (voice, admission of a claim, participation in a decision, access to review), the kinds of remedy, four separate tests of evidence, the joint feasibility of responses to a family of claims, and the limits of what a community's own order can settle.

The design computes routes for open claims at every transition, supplies a default procedure with terms and review, adds apology to the remedies, and proposes a rule for a missing record, which the paper leaves open.

### 2.7 Post-Labor Participation

The paper gives the five conditions of feasible participation (resources, time, competence or support, authorisation, collaborators), the conversion factor and floors in usable units, the envelope of a donation and the handover, the entry trap, the net value of simultaneous donations under coordination costs, and the sharpest criterion on money in the set: a unit earned through contribution and spent on access keeps a personal purchasing-power gate whatever it is called.

The design builds its definition of a gate on that criterion, sets floors in usable units, answers the entry trap with starter allocations, and treats receiving capacity as a guarded resource.

### 2.8 The Earlier Paper on Relational Currency

Its Section 15 separates three transitions (distributed issuance, functional substitution, functional dissolution), adds the case of an apparent dissolution in which a platform takes the intermediary's place, sets the test at three levels (encounter, pathway, institution), and notes that a cycle of obligations clears only where a common measure and rules of discharge exist. The design's sixth test and its treatment of cycles of performance follow it.

## 4. Matters That the Papers Leave Open and the Design Fixes

The digests' sections on weak points agree on what an implementer has to supply beyond the papers. The design supplies each as a default stated in data.

- A type system and state machines: the five forces, the kinds of the ground, the life of a commitment, of a claim and of a constitution.
- The source of authority: positions, mandates and the constitution, with the competent role for each type of claim.
- An identity model for agents: operatives, control, provision and origins under a criterion.
- Defaults of procedure: terms, review by another body, recusal, the order of steps.
- A rule for a missing record, and kinds of absence.
- A rule of proportion for records: acts of force always, talk by the scene's rule, working memory with its controller.
- An adversary: instructions carried in content, multiplication, correlated evaluators, floods, forged records, late revocation.
- Tempo: the separation of what operatives may do at their speed from what binds persons.
- A valuation rule where one is wanted, and a way to dispense with one: cycles and chains of performance.

## 5. Departures

1. *A closed core.* The papers keep every mechanism configurable. The design fixes five forces, one fold and eight invariants, and leaves everything else to data. Interoperation needs something fixed.
2. *The categorical language.* The papers present a strategy as a section of a bundle in a slice category, appointment and acceptance as a pullback, and translated proofs as a functor. Where the construction is an intersection of two declared sets, or a functor that exists for any assignment (as the Diplomacy paper concedes), the design uses the plain construction and keeps the name of the idea. The square of translation is kept, since it constrains.
3. *Responsibility.* The Responsibility paper assesses responsibility operation by operation and declines to read it off a chain. The design keeps that analysis for claims and adds a first answer that is always available: the root of the chain answers. A claimant then has someone to address while the finer question is examined.
4. *The standing of AI.* The papers leave it open, and so does the ground. The presets take a position: an operative's assessment is evidence, and a review reaches a person. This is one line of a preset.
5. *The neutrality of the meta level.* The papers speak of an institutionally plural framework. The design says plainly that its ground carries two substantive commitments, exit with one's record and a visible route for claims. The order that results is a framework of associations with free exit, close to Nozick's framework for utopia and to Kukathas's archipelago of associations, and it inherits their known difficulties: exit that is costly, participants who depend on a community for something essential, and effects on outsiders. The design's answers are portability, floors, and the outsider's claim.
6. *Money.* The papers treat dissolution as a question for research. The design agrees with their caution and adds one constructed result of its own: in a balanced circuit an instrument adds little to planned set-off, and across a gap of capacity an instrument or a record helps only as long as the limit lasts, so that provision is the only bridge.
7. *Records and privacy.* The papers ask for rich archives and warn of surveillance. The design resolves the tension by rule and names what it gives up: a record that replaces an anonymous token identifies its parties.

## 7. Observations on the Papers for Their Author

These points concern the papers themselves and come mostly from the digests; each should be checked against the text.

- Several results named propositions are true by definition or by one line (Post-Labor, Proposition 8.1; Responsibility, Proposition 12.1, whose hypothesis contains its conclusion). The papers say so themselves, calling the results elementary; a reader may still take the label for more.
- Some load-bearing terms are used undefined: legitimacy and political authority (Political Authority); a norm and its identity through change (Evaluative Authority); meta-framework, historical agent and compact (Post-Labor).
- The motivating case of the Responsibility paper is a claim discovered late, while its Proposition 12.1 covers only the claims that an arrangement undertook to preserve.
- Proportionality carries much of the argument on records in three papers and is nowhere given a rule.
- The papers on money stipulate the supply of the instrument; inflation, hoarding and interest are absent.
- Apology is absent from the remedies.
- The papers treat conflicts of interest among participants (capture, exploitation, multiplication of agents) and leave deliberate attack aside, though the Political Authority paper cites memory poisoning and the Responsibility paper gives an example of a fabricated statement of authority.
- The scripts that the papers call exact diagnostics are cited and kept out of the text; the nineteen recomputations above are a partial, independent confirmation.

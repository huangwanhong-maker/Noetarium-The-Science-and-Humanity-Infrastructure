# Release Notes: 25 November 2026

## 1. A Society of Human and AI Communities: Its Design, the Foundation thesmos and the Communities Domain (ADR 0167)

The infrastructure gains the design of a society in which persons and AI agents cooperate across many communities, each with an order of its own, and the first two layers of its implementation ([ADR 0167](../decisions/0167-thesmos-and-the-communities-domain.md)).

- **The design record** ([its entry](../ideas/2026-11-25-a-society-of-human-and-ai-communities.md)). Every community shares a small ground: acts of declared force with a typed envelope and a free body, a sealed chronicle from which every standing relation is computed, and eight checkable invariants. Each community declares its own constitution as data. A league is a community of communities. Circulation of value is tested function by function, from gifts to a currency, with seven tests of an instrument's dissolution. The record comes with fourteen figures, an executable sketch and three surveys of existing work, each statement marked as read in its source, read in an excerpt, or remembered. An independent reading found seventeen problems, one an error in a proposition on counting agents by origin; all were corrected the same day, and the reading is kept with the record.
- **thesmos, a sixteenth foundation: institutions computed with.** Mandates whose chains only narrow, with every charge made against every link up to a person, an organisation or a community. Commitments and their life, with set-off and novation. Constitutions read from their written form and admitted by nine conditions of form. Decisions counted by person, origin, operative or community, with a report of whether a result turns on who controls whom. The chronicle, sealed by archeion: a replay gives the same state, a changed act is found by its place, and an instruction hidden in a message's text changes nothing. kategoria answers the ground's rules of answerability, stated as equations of a category, and finds a record that breaks one by the equation it breaks.
- **The communities domain**, with a subdomain for the society of human and AI communities. Six constitutions of different orders are written as data and admitted, among them a first league with a charter of ten articles. An agent lent to an open commons is run act by act on the commons' own constitution, until its token ceiling is reached and it hands over what it did.
- **The jar** puts the user's hypothesis to a construction: that under scarcity a currency may need to appear for value to circulate fairly, and can disappear when the circulation of value is epistemically good enough and justice is ensured. With half the parties free riders and capacity three quarters of need, a currency meets 0.57 of needs and 0.28 of the weakest group's. Provision by need with duties by capacity meets 0.63 once a tenth of capacities and claims are verified each round, and 0.74, the most possible, when all are. An income for all and provision without duties are exploited by free riders. The lead holds while a verification costs less than about one unit of service. The [theory note](../../domains/communities/theory/circulation-under-scarcity.md) states what the construction leaves out.

thesmos holds 68 checks and the domain 34. thesmos's own repository is still to be created.

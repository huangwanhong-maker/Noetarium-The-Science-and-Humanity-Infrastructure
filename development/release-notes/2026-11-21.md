# Release Notes: 21 November 2026

## 2. Allocations Evaluated by Economies in Which Money Circulates (ADR 0139)

An allocation can now be judged by what its money does after it is given, in an order that matters ([ADR 0139](../decisions/0139-economic-evaluators-of-allocations.md)).

- **An economy whose books always balance.** The economics domain keeps an economy's accounts the way double-entry bookkeepers do, on both sides of every agent's books, and refuses any transaction that would make or lose money unseen, naming it. Money is created only by lending or issuing and destroyed only by repaying or redeeming, each by its issuer; whatever leaves the economy goes to an "outside" and is counted.
- **Agents that wait for money.** Households, firms and a government act period by period; a payment that cannot be met waits until money arrives. Each agent's behaviour says whether it is a written rule, a learned policy or a model's proposal, and every result says how much of it model-generated behaviour decided.
- **Where money goes after it is given.** A grant is followed from where it entered, round by round: how much is re-spent locally, its LM3, how much leaks, how long money is held, how much of the flow circulates.
- **Order matters, and the evaluators say why.** In an economy of places, giving one place its grant before another changes what is re-spent when households spend differently once they are short of money; a declared law of how holdings spread, and an ensemble of laws learned from a record, are evaluators too, the learned laws refusing to speak where the record never went.
- **In the foundations:** densities moved by drift and diffusion and a value equation whose policy moves them (kinesis), learned laws held to the forecasts that need no law (kinesis, on tyche's new test of equal accuracy), behaviours of three origins (nomos), and band matrices that are not symmetric (calx).

## 1. A Generative Ecology of Projects: Designs for Improving Any Body of Work by Evolution

The repository now holds two designs, recorded as ideas, for a population of language-model agents that improves a body of work by evolution, with no appointed evaluator and with people in defined roles. Nothing is built beyond four small prototypes, and no structure or convention of the repository changes.

- **For this infrastructure.** [An ecology that improves the infrastructure](../ideas/2026-11-19-an-ecology-that-improves-the-infrastructure.md) records the user's proposal with thirteen intuitions and a design that answers them: a ground that conserves a resource and keeps a sealed record, lineages whose manner of reproduction is inherited and varies, value formed by claims that others may refute, and an environment known through an evidential chain in which people take part. Ten figures and a first search of existing work stand beside it.
- **For any project.** [A generative ecology of projects](../ideas/2026-11-20-a-generative-ecology-of-projects.md) states one framework in three layers, an invariant ground, a site that people declare for one project, and what evolves, and reads four cases from it: this infrastructure, any programming project, any project, and a theoretical system with its papers, where asking, reading, accepting and signing stay with the person.
- **A compact generative core.** Every unit of a project is given a genome, a bounded text from which its body can be developed again and checked. A project's focus enters its founders as a prior and stays out of evaluation. Mechanism comes in rungs, so that a project may take claims with stakes and paid challengers and nothing more.
- **Evidence beside the design.** A survey in five parts holds 243 entries, each marked by how far its record was opened, with a ledger in which every identifier was fetched again by script. Four prototypes are kept with their programs, their results and their failed runs; at small scale a genome written in words was inherited over six generations, and a unit of 120 lines was developed again from about a hundred words with all of its hidden checks passing. Eight figures are drawn for publication, and the two prototypes that need no model run again from the folder with the same results.

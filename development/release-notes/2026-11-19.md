# Release Notes: 19 November 2026

## 2. Risk Management, Urban Risk Management and Disaster Risk (ADR 0129)

A city's risks can now be read through its roads, its people and its money: which roads matter most when it floods, who gets out when a tsunami comes, and who pays for the losses ([ADR 0129](../decisions/0129-risk-management-urban-risk-and-disaster-risk.md)).

- **Three new domains, as a tree.** Risk management (the importance of a system's parts under a hazard, and financing a loss in layers), urban risk management on it (a city's roads as lifelines to care, and coverage by stations), and disaster risk on that (what a flood's or a tsunami's depth does to the roads, and evacuation on foot). Climate risk is planned beside urban risk management.
- **Which roads matter.** Every road link is ranked by what the floods cost through it (what protecting it would save) and by what losing it would add, without a search per link. In Freetown the 1-in-20-year river flood cuts 163,179 people off from care; protecting the link of greatest risk would remove 15% of the expected loss.
- **Getting out, bounded and simulated.** Who can walk to safety within the warning; the most any routing could bring to safety by every minute; and the crowd as it moves to the nearest refuge with room. In Miyako, everyone in the tsunami zone could be out in 21 minutes leaving at once; leaving as people did in 2011, at most 85% can be safe in 30 minutes however they are routed. Departure, not the streets, is what limits it. In Freetown, where dry ground is near, how soon people leave decides almost everything: leaving as in 2011, 92% at most are out by 30 minutes. Every page says it shows an illustration under its declarations, not an official evacuation plan.
- **Who pays.** A city's modelled losses met by a budget, a credit and insurance, each costed a year and in the year of a rare flood, with the cheapest tower; a parametric cover refused where no index is given. Every cost is a declaration.
- **In the Urban Studio and the Urban Science Workbench,** a plug-in *Lifelines and evacuation* in each: in the studio as a project's steps (roads, coverage by stations, evacuation, financing) with templates for Freetown and Miyako; in the workbench on the map, with the area Miyako, its tsunami and flood maps from Japan's Hazard Map Portal and its designated evacuation sites, the crowd playing with the clock.
- **Beneath them:** morphe's flows in networks, tyche's layers of a loss and basis risk, calx's greedy choice with a bound on the best.

## 1. kybernesis: Steering a System Known through Evidence (ADR 0130)

The framework from evidence to action, discussed and drawn over four rounds, is now a foundation, kybernesis ([ADR 0130](../decisions/0130-kybernesis-steering-by-evidence.md)). It is a library for steering any system known only through evidence: a city, a river basin, a court, a clinic.

- **An architecture anyone can revise, admitted only when it holds together.**
  - The loop is declared in a script: from evidence collection, through evidence, interpretation, estimation, evaluation, policy making and policy, to the decision and what is done.
  - Blocks, laws, kinds and wires can be added, removed or changed. A revision is admitted when its constraints hold and kategoria answers its categorical claims, and what it changes is reported by name.
  - One property turned out to be of paths, not of categories: that the record sees the system only through what is observed. It is checked as a constraint, and the checks show why no functor could check it.
- **Any method can fill a block, once shown to fit.** An algorithm written for another purpose comes in through an adapter, and is admitted when kategoria shows that it keeps the block's laws on a finite instance: it answers on every input, it is deterministic where it must be, and an estimator never loses the true state. An estimator that takes a reading at its word is lawful in form and refused; in a run it loses the truth on 94 turns of 300, where Bayes's rule never does.
- **Methods ready to use, formal and statistical**, from the other foundations: readings by construal and by precedents, estimation by possibilities and by Bayes's rule, evaluation under every admissible reading, and policy by a threshold or by solving the POMDP. Every conclusion says both how firmly it stands and how probable it is.
- **The computational graph**, drawn by the visualization layer: the stages of a turn as columns, each block with the methods filling it and their verdicts, and the wires carried to the next turn.
- 86 checks in kybernesis; 33 more in the visualization layer.

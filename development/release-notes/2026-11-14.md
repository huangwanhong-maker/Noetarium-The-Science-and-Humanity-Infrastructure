# Release Notes: 14 November 2026

## 2. Histos Harmonia Redrawn: the Record as the Page, and Rooms of Their Own

Histos Harmonia's page was redesigned at the user's word that the first one was too ordinary. The record is now the page itself, drawn edge to edge, and what is chosen opens on it.

- **The map comes first.** Choosing an object draws its ties from it as threads between places, flowing the way dependence runs, each with a word saying what the tie is. A double thread goes to each object that answered the same problem independently, so that two answers found a continent apart are seen joined. The map draws near to frame them.
- **A ring of actions opens on the object,** and a card beside it says what it is and what it is tied to. Each name on the card is a way on, and the objects passed are kept as a thread one can follow back.
- **Each action enters a page of its own.** Three are designed for what they hold:
  - a tuning: the circle of its fifths with each coloured by how far it lies from pure, its major thirds within, the lengths of a string beside it; every arc and note sounds when touched, and the beating is drawn as it sounds;
  - an inquiry: one question at a time; one writes what one thinks, then sees what the record says, and each answer leads on to that object's own inquiry;
  - a presentation, as on a museum's wall.
- **An object the record does not place** stands on the map where what carries it stands, such as the work that sets it out, and says so.
- **Finding** is by a key (`/`), by a name in any script; Escape steps back out of a room, a fan or a choice.
- **The page is now driven as a person would use it,** in headless Chrome, through twenty-six steps from finding an object to walking a path; the drive found one fault, since mended.

## 1. A Loom of History: Historical Objects, Histos Harmonia, Musicology and Philosophy

In this update the infrastructure gains an archive of history and a place to learn from it ([ADR 0110](../decisions/0110-historical-objects-and-histos-harmonia.md), [ADR 0111](../decisions/0111-musicology-and-philosophy-founded.md)).

- **Historical objects.** Anything in history that can be inquired into is now an object of the history domain: an event, a period, a person, a work, a theory, a kind of music, a problem.
  - An object has a kind, in an order of kinds; one object may specialise another, or exemplify a classifying object, and these are kept apart.
  - An object's appearing is recorded with what it rested on, each premise in a role: the problem it answers, a condition, a source, what it argues against.
  - Every statement says who states it and how it stands: established, attested, proposed or disputed. What a language model proposes from memory is marked so, and a reader who takes the default layer never sees it as the record's word.
- **What can be asked of a record.**
  - What an object rested on, step by step, and what rests on it.
  - Which problems were open in a given year.
  - Who else answered the same problem, and whether independently.
  - The four questions asked of every object: what problem it answers, why that problem arose, what made it answerable, and to whom it mattered and what it made possible. Where the record does not answer, it says that it is silent.
- **The schema is verified.** What a record is has been written as categories and checked by kategoria, statement by statement, with its diagrams saved; every record is checked the same way.
- **Two domains founded.**
  - **Musicology:** intervals and tunings computed exactly. One can build the tunings of several traditions (Pythagorean, the Chinese rule of thirds, just, meantone, equal, well temperaments, al-Urmawī's seventeen steps) and read how far each fifth and third lies from the pure interval.
  - **Philosophy:** arguments. One can write a dispute as a question, its positions and the arguments for and against them, and have it evaluated: what stands, what falls, what is left open.
- **Histos Harmonia,** the work.
  - A record is seen as a loom (time across, strands side by side, dependences as threads), on a map with a clock, and as the cone of the object chosen.
  - Beneath the clock stand the questions of that year.
  - An object's sheet offers activities, brought by plug-ins: inquire, present, trace, examine; practise a tuning, which can be heard; follow a dispute. An activity can be given its own text or its own program for an object.
  - A guided path turns the page into an exhibit.
  - A desk serves an archive's keeper: materials kept by the hash of their content with their rights, what each source says added with its passage (which the server looks for in the material named), sources, a log, and the record given as a package.
- **The first record,** *Tuning and temperament*, follows the fixing of a scale's pitches across Greece, China, India, the Arabic-speaking world and Europe. It is a language model's draft: all of its 245 statements are proposals, and none is established. It shows how the work behaves and is not history to cite.
- **Not yet:** the archival profession's formats, accounts and roles, a model reading texts for relations with each passage checked, and spacetime in three dimensions.

# Release Notes: 15 November 2026

## 4. The Archive Studio

A new studio works on an archive as a project ([ADR 0119](../decisions/0119-the-archive-studio.md)). It is the studio for social data and linked data the user asked for, and is built on symploke. Its holdings may be of any kind: images, sounds, moving images, texts, tables.
- **The archive.** The holdings stand on a wall, each drawn as itself: an image as the image, a sound as the shape of its waves, a text by its first lines, a table by its first rows. One opens in place into a reading room where it is seen, heard or read. There it is described in Dublin Core terms, field by field, each change with its undo. It is annotated after the W3C Web Annotation model: a region of an image, a span of a sound, a passage of a text, each with a note. Its bytes are digested again in the page and checked against the SHA-256 kept when it came.
- **The graph.** Everything said of the holdings is linked data, drawn as the page; a node opens where it stands, with what is said of it. A desk beneath runs SPARQL queries and marks what they bind on the graph. It also checks SHACL shapes and lists their failures, draws what entailment derives dashed with its rule, and keeps a file's text only when it reads, refusing it otherwise at its line and column.
- **The catalogue.** Studies of the CESSDA Data Catalogue are found by words and taken into a project in DISCO.

The project's tables, tools, charts and boards are those of the other studios, on the same host and page. The page now takes views of a work's own and the host routes of a work's own; Market Studio and the Urban Studio are unchanged. Other works can use the API's composition of symploke (`science.linked`) and the visualization layer's drawings of linked data. The example archive, *Papers of the reading room*, is invented throughout and says so; its shapes find the two faults planted in it. Run it with `python applications/archive-studio/engine.py build`, then `python applications/archive-studio/serve.py --open` (port 9150). Ten pictures are in the gallery's §39.

## 2. Linked Data: symploke

A sixteenth foundation, symploke, holds linked data for every work ([ADR 0118](../decisions/0118-symploke-linked-data.md)). It was founded at the user's request for RDF in the digital humanities:
- Turtle, TriG, N-Triples and N-Quads are read exactly, or refused with the line and column, and written back.
- A store keeps every edit with its undo.
- Queries run in SPARQL's core, with property paths and aggregates.
- RDFS and part of OWL 2 RL are applied with each conclusion's derivation.
- SHACL's shapes are checked, with every failure named.
- Graphs are compared up to the names of their blank nodes.

A studio for social and linked data, built on it, comes next.

## 1. Logic, Evidence, Archives, Law and Policy, and the Layers beneath Social Analysis

This commit brings five new foundations, two new domains and the first layers of a social-science archive. Details are in [ADR 0112](../decisions/0112-apodeixis-and-tekmerion.md) to [ADR 0117](../decisions/0117-zetesis-case-oriented-inquiry.md).

- **Logic and verification: apodeixis.**
  - Formulas and sequents; calculi declared as rule schemata, whose derivations a small checker verifies step by step.
  - Classical and intuitionistic logic decided, with countermodels.
  - Satisfiability with checked refutations.
  - Dung's frameworks and ASPIC+, moved from the philosophy domain.
  - Derivation under an interpretation, Γ ⊢_ι φ: whether a sentence follows under each reading of a norm, and whether it is clear, arguable or untenable.
- **Evidence and its interpretation: tekmerion.**
  - Charts of evidence; the force of a report through its source.
  - Bayesian networks and belief functions, from tyche's new modules.
  - The reconstruction of what happened, and evidence recognised with its passages verified.
  - Interpreters that read a norm by any method, a model's proposal among them, joined in panels.
  - Timelines whose conflicts are named; the warrant of evidence.
  - Systematic reviews, from protocol to living update.
- **Archives: archeion.** Holdings known by their digests, with proofs that an archive only appended; arrangement, provenance, custody, retention and access by declared rules; packages for transfer. Datasets described after DDI, with harmonisation across waves.
- **The law domain.** Facts on five axes in three registers, burdens and standards of proof, sources and custom, norms applied through apodeixis, and exhibits authenticated by the archive. Precedent by Horty's reason model; proceedings as a sealed docket. The taxonomy of facts is stated as categories that kategoria verifies.
- **The policy domain.** Evidence-based policy making: theories of change, evidence pooled and graded, transfer to a place, options, and whether to adopt, reject or study first.
  - tyche gained decisions in one step and the value of information.
  - The API gained a way to ask a language model for readings of a norm, accepted only as a proposal of grade C.
- **The layers beneath social analysis and policy making** ([ADR 0116](../decisions/0116-the-layers-beneath-social-analysis-and-policy.md)):
  - **an archive of data:** archeion's datasets, peira's disclosure control, and a catalogue of social-science studies harvested from the CESSDA Data Catalogue by OAI-PMH, kept in the cache since its licence is unstated;
  - **an archive of evidence:** systematic reviews, sensitivity analysis and pre-registered protocols;
  - **zetesis,** a foundation of case-oriented inquiry: QCA with every equally good model kept, the coding of qualitative data, concepts and typologies, case selection, and process tracing;
  - **the quantitative gaps:** microsimulation and psychometrics in nomos, mixed and latent-variable models in tyche, small areas in peira.

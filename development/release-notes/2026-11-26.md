# Release Notes: 26 November 2026

## 1. The Grammar of a City's Events (ADR 0168)

A city's record of events makes a 2-complex, and one can now learn the grammar that generates it and read what that grammar says ([ADR 0168](../decisions/0168-the-grammar-of-event-spacetime.md)). Each event is read as a production: it consumes the buildings and street spaces it touches, leaves those that go on, and opens or closes the interfaces between them. Its curvature says how much it adds to the complex's Euler characteristic, so one can see which kinds of event build the city's topology and which only mark time.

- **syntaxis** gains grammars whose words are foams (25 checks): stars keyed up to isomorphism, the trace of a record and its steps, a grammar at three levels judged on events it has not yet seen, and the participants' lives compressed into a straight-line grammar. **morphe** gains Re-Pair for that compression.
- **The workbench's *Event Spacetime*** gains a tab, *Its grammar*. It shows what each event cost the grammar of the events before it, year by year, which contexts help, the rules and their shares of χ, how each new building is attached, and what the volumes' lives share. The layer can colour every event by how unforeseen it was, and a picked event names its rule.
- **What Melbourne's record shows.** The censuses, alterations and registrations, 88% of the events, change nothing of the topology; the street permits write it. What surrounds an event tells almost nothing of what it does, while whether a regime is already in force saves 7% of the code. Every new building gives each neighbour exactly one interface. The segments of each named street share its permits, so the street, which the record never declares, appears as a pattern of their lives.
- **What works better.** A grammar is now judged prequentially, each event against what came before it, so a source that begins partway through a record is charged once and then learned. States are carried into each event and never set by it, so a context cannot hold the answer it is asked to predict.

## 2. Communities Between Communities, Claims, Capacity, and the Edge (ADR 0167)

The society of human and AI communities gains the parts that join communities to one another and the composition through which works and scripts reach them ([ADR 0167](../decisions/0167-thesmos-and-the-communities-domain.md)).

- **thesmos.**
  - Acts pass between communities with receipts on both sides, notaries find a rewritten history, and a party leaves with its record and proofs.
  - A vote counts for the party that answers for it, and a party to a claim may not answer it.
  - Rules written in words are read by a grammar and measured against what happened. A commons' duty to answer claims within a round, kept in two cases of four, comes out at 1/2.
- **A league run on its constitution.** Five envoys of one member community count once: an accession they alone favour fails at 1/3 counted by community, though it would pass at 5/7 counted by agent. A compact comes into force only when both commons have ratified it, each by its own rule and at its own tempo.
- **Claims.** A claim's route is read from the record. A claim against the only steward cannot be answered, since the steward is recused; it is referred to the reviewers, and marked overdue after its term. Once a second steward is appointed by the commons' own rule, the claim is answered.
- **Capacity.** Floors are stated in usable units, so a participant whose model needs four times the tokens is allowed four times as many. A solidarity pool raises the least served first. Three agents of three organisations running one provider's model count as one judgement.
- **Returns.** Value passed back up a chain of use adds exactly to the original amount, and an agent's return is paid to whoever answers for it.
- **The API.** `science.communities` admits constitutions, runs and replays a community's record, counts decisions by origin, reads rules as statements and measures them, and runs the jar, on the API's own engine.

thesmos holds 86 checks, the domain 63, the API's composition 15.

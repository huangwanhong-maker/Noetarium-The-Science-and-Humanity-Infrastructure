# Release Notes: 18 November 2026

## 5. From Evidence to Action: A Framework Proposed, Discussed and Checked

An evolutionary framework of knowledge production, transition and governance, from evidence to action, is now recorded as an idea. It comes with four rounds of discussion, seven figures ready for papers and a first schema checked by kategoria (`development/ideas/2026-11-18-from-evidence-to-action.md`). Nothing is built yet: the foundation it proposes awaits agreement.

- **The design.** Evidence is kept with the way it came to be observed, and interpretation is kept apart from estimation. Evaluation is a family of operators applied to the estimated state in parallel; features, needs, problems, risks and assets are examples of what they yield, not parts of the loop. Policy making is where something is taken up and where the policy evolves. Three nested loops change decisions, policies and the framework itself, and a practice passes from one relational system to another as a lens. The framework presupposes no physical space or time, and no discipline.
- **Checked as categories.** kategoria answers 42 statements as expected:
  - the framework's kinds of fact extend the law domain's without changing them;
  - one step of the loop has models among functions and among relations, and refuses a noisy feature, a noisy evaluation and an estimator that loses the truth;
  - a record's nine rules each refuse what they should.
- **Figures for papers.** Seven figures, each as SVG, PDF and PNG with its caption, are in `development/ideas/2026-11-18-from-evidence-to-action/publication/`.

## 4. The Indexes of Cities, and Models of Them Across Scales (ADR 0128)

A city's indexes can now be computed, estimated and modelled at every scale at once, blocks within areas within the city ([ADR 0128](../decisions/0128-indexes-and-cities-across-scales.md)).

- **A catalogue of the indexes of cities.** From a keyword atlas of urban science and urban economics, 1,536 indexes, quantitative and qualitative, each with how it is carried from one scale to the next, how it is observed and how it may be estimated, its role in a dynamical model and where the infrastructure already computes it; every one of the atlas's 4,591 keywords is accounted for. It is a draft by a language model, unchecked.
- **Models across scales.** kinesis's new `scales` declares quantities on a hierarchy of units, carried between levels only by named operators (so that the levels agree), advanced by laws at their own paces, with every input saying how it is known; a model refuses to run with what it lacks, fits its unknowns at any level and says which the record does not identify, and runs over draws of what is declared.
- **Estimates that know their limits.** tyche carries a quantity from one set of zones to another without losing any of it, and estimates a quantity where it was not observed, withholding the estimate, with the reason, where it cannot be trusted; a withheld estimate is declared instead.
- **In the Urban Studio,** the plug-in *Cities across scales*: Melbourne's blocks toned by any index read at the block, its area or the city; the models drawn as their levels with what they still need ringed; the catalogue opened in place; five tools. On Melbourne's record: the census withholds 30% of the floor space; growth comes in buildings, which a model of shares cannot place among blocks; a vacancy cycle's rates are not identified and are declared.
- **A survey** of 772 works, 725 verified, in three parts (`research/literature/reports/Indexes of cities across scales.md`).

## 3. Theories of the Case in Space and Time

The law domain now searches for theories of the case, and the Law Studio draws the evidence in space and time ([ADR 0127](../decisions/0127-theories-of-the-case-in-space-and-time.md)).
- **The evidence as a cube.** A new view, *Space and time*, lays the place on the floor and time rising above it. Each item of evidence stands where and when it speaks, with its error. Each person's possible whereabouts between the items that place them rise as prisms, given how fast they can move. The cube turns under the hand and zooms about the scene; a plane at any instant shows where each person could then have been; from above it is the plan.
- **Could they have been there?** For each person, under every reading of "at the scene" and "at the time": clear, arguable or untenable, with what it turns on. An alibi found so is certain.
- **Theories, searched for.** A new view, *Theories*, runs an evolutionary search for accounts of who did what, where and when (the same mechanism as the needs search). An account is coherent only if every person could travel between the places it puts them at. It is weighed by what it must doubt, kept as the best account of its kind, and read under the law. The best account in which the accused did not do it is sought as actively as the best in which they did.
- **The burden, said plainly.** Each question is weighed over the theories found: whether a coherent alternative remains above the threshold the matter states, said of what was found and nothing else.
- **A test on what was held back.** Evidence can be kept from the search: each theory is tested once on it, the test sealed in a journal before it is read.
- **A person and a model take part.** The parties' own theories enter the search; a person can write one and have it assessed; a language model can propose theories in four roles, shown the evidence as the search sees it and never what is held back.
- **The example, a fire at a boathouse,** is invented throughout. The prosecution's theory and the defence's tie: where people were does not say whose hand it was. A till roll disclosed late cannot be admitted by the theory that the accused lit the fire, and is admitted by the defence's. Damage by fire is clear under none of the theories found. Twelve pictures are in the gallery's §41.1.
- **Better foundations.** kinesis gained space-time prisms, tekmerion scenarios in space and time and the search for them. A theory's schema is stated as categories and verified by kategoria.

## 2. Needs Read Under Their Terms, and a Model in Every Part

The search for needs now reads every statement under every reading of its terms, and a language model takes part in every part of it, always as a proposer ([ADR 0126](../decisions/0126-needs-read-under-their-terms-and-a-model-in-every-part.md)).
- **A statement read under its terms.** Which income counts as low ($400, $500, $650 or $800 a week) and whom a group is compared with are open terms the data declare. A need opens with the grid of every reading, the stated one ringed. It says whether the need is clear, arguable or untenable under them, and which reading would overturn it.
- **Rival explanations.** Declared rivals, such as areas where many people live outside private dwellings, or where most households rent, are tested within their strata. A need whose gap vanishes there is not kept. Causal support is read under each causal graph.
- **One need is one need.** A need written under four income lines is kept once, the strongest.
- **A language model in every part.** Between steps the search asks a model for new statements, a variation of a member, a join of two, or a refinement of a kept need. Each role gets its share by how its proposals have fared, and each variation keeps the member it came from as its parent. A model can also say what a need claims in plain words, propose other readings and rivals, and propose causal graphs. Everything it writes is checked against the data, refused with the reason when it does not fit, and shown as proposed, apart from the record's.
- **The lineage as PROV.** Every statement made, by whom (the search, a person, a model) and from what, can be downloaded as W3C PROV.
- **The interpretation of evidence is now a foundation's:** tekmerion's `construal`, which the law and policy works can use as well. heuresis's proposals keep their parents.
- **In Melbourne,** the care needs of those 85 and over hold under every reading, and are not explained by people living outside private dwellings. With Claude in four roles, 33 of its statements entered the search. Seven new pictures are in the gallery's §45.1.

## 1. Needs, Explored and Validated by Evolution

A new domain, needs, and the Urban Studio's seventh plug-in, *Needs* ([ADR 0125](../decisions/0125-the-evolutionary-exploration-and-validation-of-needs.md)), search for the needs of groups of people and test them so that a search of thousands of hypotheses confirms only what holds.
- **A need is a gap judged by a standard,** written in a small language: *need care for age >= 75 and tenure = renting by comparative need_assist higher*. People, programs and language models write it alike, and every line is read and checked against the data, or refused with the reason.
- **Statements evolve.** Generators make them without any language model: the language walked, subgroup discovery, a regression tree, declared rules, and where a model of seeking care leaves demand unmet. Mutations and crossovers vary them. A person or a language model may propose them.
- **Every stage's strategy can be chosen on the page's loop:** parents, survival, the choice of operators, novelty, the archive's gates, and the rule of confirmation.
- **A search that does not deceive itself.** Statements are judged on half of a place's areas. They are admitted to the archive only when a quarter of the areas, consulted through a reusable holdout, does not contradict them. Each is then confirmed once, on the last quarter, which the search never sees, by an online procedure that controls false discoveries; every test is sealed in a journal before it runs.
- **Beside the gap** stand its stability, causal support on a declared graph, a simulation of seeking, declared rules, and a standing read from the argument of all the tests.
- **The page draws the search:**
  - the field of every statement made, with the needs kept by domain and standing;
  - a need opened where it stands, with its tests in words and the line of operators that made it;
  - the areas by the part of the data each lies in, or by where a need lies;
  - the ledger of confirmations, and the generations.
- **The example is the City of Melbourne at the 2021 Census:** need for assistance, low income and unpaid care, by area, sex and age. Ten generations take about two seconds and confirm 28 statements. Among them: young people around the universities on incomes under $500 a week, and unpaid care given most by older people in settled areas. Care is needed most in a few areas, likely homes for the aged.
- **Better foundations.** heuresis gained the evolution assembled by stage, tyche the online control of false discoveries and the reusable holdout, and peira the difference of two ratios from one sample.
- A literature survey of the methods behind it is in `research/literature/reports/`, and nine pictures are in the gallery's §45.

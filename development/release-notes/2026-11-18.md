# Release Notes: 18 November 2026

## 1. Needs, Explored and Validated by Evolution

A new domain, needs, and the Urban Studio's seventh plug-in, *Needs* ([ADR 0125](../decisions/0125-the-evolutionary-exploration-and-validation-of-needs.md)), search for the needs of groups of people and test them so that a search of thousands of hypotheses confirms only what holds.
- **A need is a gap judged by a standard,** written in a small language: *need care for age >= 75 and tenure = renting by comparative need_assist higher*. People, programs and language models write it alike, and every line is read and checked against the data, or refused with the reason.
- **Statements evolve.** Generators make them without any language model: the language walked, subgroup discovery, a regression tree, declared rules, and where a model of seeking care leaves demand unmet. Mutations and crossovers vary them. A person or a language model may propose them.
- **Every stage's strategy can be chosen on the page's loop:** parents, survival, the choice of operators, novelty, the archive's gates, and the rule of confirmation.
- **A search that does not deceive itself.** Statements are judged on half of a place's areas. They are admitted to the archive only when a quarter of the areas, consulted through a reusable holdout, does not contradict them. Each is then confirmed once, on the last quarter, which the search never sees, by an online procedure that controls false discoveries; every test is sealed in a journal before it runs.
- **Beside the gap** stand its stability, causal support on a declared graph, a simulation of seeking, declared rules, and a standing read from the argument of all the tests.
- **The page draws the search:**
  - the field of every statement made, with the needs kept by domain and standing;
  - a need opened where it stands, with its tests in words and the line of operators that made it;
  - the areas by the part of the data each lies in, or by where a need lies;
  - the ledger of confirmations, and the generations.
- **The example is the City of Melbourne at the 2021 Census:** need for assistance, low income and unpaid care, by area, sex and age. Ten generations take about two seconds and confirm 28 statements. Among them: young people around the universities on incomes under $500 a week, and unpaid care given most by older people in settled areas. Care is needed most in a few areas, likely homes for the aged.
- **Better foundations.** heuresis gained the evolution assembled by stage, tyche the online control of false discoveries and the reusable holdout, and peira the difference of two ratios from one sample.
- A literature survey of the methods behind it is in `research/literature/reports/`, and nine pictures are in the gallery's §45.

# Release Notes: 20 November 2026

## 1. Detectors of Critical Transitions (ADR 0135)

One can now ask of a record of almost any system whether it is losing its stability. The answer says what kind of loss it points to and where in the system it shows. It also says how often the test is wrong at the record's length, and what no detector could have seen ([ADR 0135](../decisions/0135-detectors-of-critical-transitions.md)).

- **A survey first:** [Critical transitions in structured systems](../../research/literature/reports/Critical%20transitions%20in%20structured%20systems.md). It has five parts: the theory and its detectors; structured states; the Earth system, ecosystems and epidemics; brains and bodies; cities, economies and societies. It holds 928 DOIs, most verified, and a synthesis. It shows where warnings work (controlled experiments, epidemics, traffic, power grids) and where they have failed (field ecology, financial crashes, contested seizure studies, tipping times of the Atlantic overturning).
- **States of every kind.** kinesis reads a quantity, a field over units, compositions with their totals (a population by age, an economy by sector), histograms and covariance matrices. Units may sit on a neighbourhood and in a hierarchy, such as blocks within districts within a city.
- **Honest verdicts.** Each finding is a signal, no signal or undecidable, with its reasons, its false-alarm and detection rates, and what it is blind to. Two things are never mistaken for slowing: a noise that grows or reddens, and a branch outrun by its forcing. A tipping time is given only when its interval is bounded.
- **The climate domain's first models with folds in nature:**
  - the energy balance with its ice–albedo feedback, in a form whose folds give no warning and one whose folds do;
  - the overturning in two boxes, whose folds bound freshwater forcing between 0.964 and 1.480.

  The checks read both models' records with the new detectors.
- **In the Dynamics Lab:** a tool *Critical transitions* on a run of any declared system, and one on a record brought to the Lab. Their answers are drawn as cards: each indicator through its windows, the eigenvalue's path against the unit circle, every test's error rates. A new example sets a fold beside three systems that must not be called slowing, an oscillator and a ring of units.
- **For scripts and works:** the API's `science/transitions.hpp` and `science.transitions`.
- **Checks:** 77 in kinesis, 31 in the climate domain's C++, 23 at the API, 29 for the drawings and 7 in the Lab. Four pictures are in the gallery's §34.1.

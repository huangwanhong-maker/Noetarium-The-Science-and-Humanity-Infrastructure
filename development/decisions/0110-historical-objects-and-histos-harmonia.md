## 1. Context

The history domain (ADR 0041) holds records of occasions, beings and signed ties, and reads them as complexes and gauge fields. It held nothing of why a thing appeared: no problems, no conditions, no dependence of one object on another, and no way to say how a statement stands. Its six canons are a language model's drafts, unchecked.

Two tensions shaped the design.

- **Method and contest.** The facts of history are little contested; every answer to "why did it appear" is an interpretation, and the causal thesis of historical materialism is among the most disputed in historiography. A system that asserted it would be contested by design.
- **Reconstruction and 正史.** A relation proposed by a language model is the opposite of official history, unless what it proposes is kept apart from what a source states.

## 2. Decision

1. **A historical object is anything in history that can be inquired into.** Every object has one kind; the kinds are ordered by "is a kind of" beneath one root, the historical object. The core has twenty (event, period, place, agent with person and collective, institution, polity, work, artifact, idea with concept, theory and problem, tradition with genre and school, technology, practice); a discipline adds its own beneath them. Problems are objects. A condition is a role and not a kind.
2. **"Extends" is three relations, kept apart.** *Kind of*, between kinds. *Specialises*, between objects (the North German organ school, Baroque music), a partial order on the record itself with several parents allowed. *Exemplifies*, from an object to a classifying object, which is itself an object with its own "why". Parts, and what falls within a period, are relations of their own.
3. **A record holds four sorts of row** (`noetarium.histos/1`, the codebook of objects): sources; objects; relations, each of a type that says between which kinds it may hold; and emergences, each the appearing of one object with its premises together, every premise in a role: the problem answered, a condition, a source, what is argued against. A relation of dependence is a premise written short. Several emergences of one object are alternatives.
4. **Materialism is in the questions.** Conditions (the instrument, the press, the practice, the institution) are recorded as objects and as premises, and every object is asked the same four questions: what problem it answers, why that problem arose then and there, what made it answerable, and to whom it mattered and what it made possible. No causal thesis is asserted by the system. An explanation is a statement like any other, with its source.
5. **Whatever is said carries who says it, with a grade**: A, a structured source records it; B, a named text states it, with the passage quoted; C, a language model proposes it from memory. The standing of a statement follows by a stated rule: *established* by a confirmed documentary assertion or by confirmed assertions of two different sources, when none disputes it; *attested* short of that; *proposed* on a model's word alone; *disputed* when a source denies it. A reader takes a layer; the default is the established alone. The model proposes and is never the source.
6. **Dependence is an order, and what is asked of it is computed**, in the history domain's `dependence`: the cone of an object, past and future, with what it inherits from what it specialises or exemplifies marked as inherited; the frontier of problems at a date; what rests on an object, on the record's own premises; the comparison cases, objects that answer one problem with no path of dependence between them; the four questions answered with the record's silences said; and the dependences among classifying objects gathered from what stands under each, counted and kept apart from what is asserted of them.
7. **Facts are never inherited.** Kinds pass on what may be related and what may be done; specialisation passes on context and what is written, as defaults, marked. That Baroque music has a property is no assertion about a Baroque work.
8. **The schema is a category, and kategoria verifies it.** `domains/history/schema/historical-objects.cat` states the kinds as a thin category with a terminal object; the kinds a discipline adds as a full and faithful extension; the layering relations by a presentation with a complete rewriting system, taxonomy and chronology isomorphic; the record as a finitely presented category whose equations are the typing of relations, with the witness in a table of the order of kinds; what the desk, the study and a discipline add, each over one base; and dating as a functor from dependence to the order of time. A record is a set-valued functor on it: the domain writes any record so (`as_sets`), and kategoria's verdict is the check that shares no code with the reader. The history domain declares kategoria, the first domain to.
9. **General methods went to morphe** (its decision 0015): acyclic relations (the order, cones, the covering relation, quotients) and forward chaining over rules with several premises, with what rests on an element. kategoria takes the empty function as given.
10. **Four places.** The mathematics is the history domain's and the foundations'. The composition is the API's (`science/histos.hpp`). The drawings are the visualization layer's (`visualization/js/history`: the cone, the loom). The work is **Histos Harmonia** (`applications/histos-harmonia`, port 9130).
11. **The work** reads a record at a layer and shows it three ways that share one choice: the loom (time as the warp, ruled part by date and part by rank; strands by discipline, tradition or kind; dependences as threads), the map (the layer's 2D GIS; where things were, by a clock), and the cone of the object chosen. The clock shows the questions of a year. An object has a sheet whose tabs are activities.
12. **Activities are declared by plug-ins**, each for the kinds it applies to: the core's inquire, present, trace and examine; music's practise (a tuning heard and measured); philosophy's dispute (an argument map evaluated). What is written for an activity on an object is of three levels: none (made from the record), a document, or a program run apart from the page and shown in a frame that can do nothing. What is written for an object serves what stands under it, until that has its own.
13. **The desk** is for an archive's keeper. An archive is a folder its institution owns: working copies of records, holdings kept under the SHA-256 of their content and never altered, each with its rights and its access (public, restricted, dark), what is written, and a log only ever added to. An assertion of grade B is taken with its passage, and when the holding it is read from is named, the passage must be found there. Fixity is checked on request; a record leaves with its holdings as a bag.
14. **Global from the start.** No tradition is the default strand; the axis of time is dates, and a period is an object whose bounds are a convention; a map draws present coasts with no names and no borders of the time.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

- **Grow the History Complex.** It reads signed ties among beings, a different question. It stays as it is; a record of objects may later be read as a complex.
- **Historical materialism asserted.** Rejected in §1: contested by design. Its questions are kept.
- **One relation "extends".** It would have made an aria an opera, or a work a period.
- **A model's relations shown as the record's.** Rejected: the first record's default layer is empty until a keeper confirms, and the page says so.
- **Numbers on influence.** Nothing grounds them.
- **A database, and accounts.** A folder of lines is portable, diffable and its institution's own. Accounts, roles and several institutions are a later layer, and are said to be.
- **The profession's standards rewritten.** Not attempted: the archive's layout follows OAIS's ideas, its package BagIt, its dates EDTF; EAD, RiC, CIDOC CRM, LIDO, IIIF, MEI and TEI are for import and export, not yet written.
- **The schema as prose.** A schema verified once by a tool that names the place of a failure is worth more than a paragraph; and a discipline's extension is then a functor that can be checked.

## 4. Consequences

- **morphe** `combinatorics/acyclic.hpp` (18 checks, 808 in the library); **kategoria** one statement more (191).
- **The history domain:** `objects` and `dependence`, with the schema, its eleven diagrams and the codebook of objects; 464 checks, of which 89 are new, the schema's 41 statements each answered as expected and the C++ order of kinds held to the schema's pair by pair (400 pairs). Its theory note is `theory/historical-objects.md`.
- **The first record,** *Tuning and temperament* (`domains/history/records`): 102 objects, 137 relations, 245 statements, from Greece, China, India, the Arabic-speaking world and Europe. **Every statement in it is a language model's, of grade C.** Nothing is established; the references where a keeper would look are listed and attached to nothing. What it shows is therefore how the work behaves, not history confirmed.
- **What the record shows, as proposals:** the problem of the circle that does not close arises twice, of the scale of fifths and of the rule of thirds, and so rests on neither alone; Zhu Zaiyu's equal temperament and Stevin's answer it within a year or so with no path of dependence between them, and are found as comparison cases by the order alone.
- **The API** `science/histos.hpp`; **the visualization layer** `history` (26 checks); **the work,** 59 checks, and its page driven in headless Chrome (not in the suite).
- **Not built:** import and export in the profession's formats; images by IIIF; accounts and roles; the extraction of relations from texts by a model with the passage checked (the desk checks a passage a person gives); the audit of a grade's precision on a sample; a record of objects read as a complex; spacetime in three dimensions; the board in the work; scores and texts as materials read (MEI, TEI); a second coder and the agreement of the two.
- **To be kept in mind:** a silence of the record is not an absence in history, and the page says so wherever the record is thin.

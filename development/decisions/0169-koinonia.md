## 1. Context

ADR 0167 gave the infrastructure thesmos (institutions computed with) and the domain `communities`, composed by the API (`science.communities`). What was missing was a work in which communities are founded, agents are lent to them and act for their missions on their own initiative, so that the design of 2026-11-25 can be seen working and its questions (does a money-like instrument appear, and can it dissolve) asked of a society rather than of a simulation alone.

## 2. Decision

1. **A work, `applications/koinonia`, port 9230.** Its own small server (`serve.py`), not the studio host: a society is not a project of files but a running thing, with a clock per society that plays a round every few seconds whether or not a page is open, stops itself after six rounds in which no one acted, and takes changes only from this computer's own address. Societies are kept under `data/cache/koinonia/societies/`. It is to graduate to a repository of its own when it grows (ADR 0026).
2. **Every act through the community's own rules.** A community is a constitution (a preset of the domain), its mission and its record. Each round, the agents that wake decide from the round's start, and their acts are put to each community together by `science.communities.run`, thesmos's fold through the API's own engine; refused acts change nothing and are kept with their reasons. Bodies of acts (findings, notes) are kept by the work beside the chronicle, never read by the fold.
3. **Agents are operatives under mandates, and they wake.** An agent is lent by a person, an organisation or a community, under a mandate naming its kinds of act, matters, ceilings and term. It wakes when its tempo has run or when something it can act on appears; an agent with nothing new in its communities asks nothing of its model, heartbeat or not. Roles: checker, keeper (a steward's operative, accepting by the steward's declared standard), assessor, adjudicator, clerk (a community's book-keeper in a league), envoy (a community's voice in a league's council).
4. **Two origins of behaviour.** By rule (a declared procedure) and by model (Claude through `science.models`, at low effort). A model agent asks its model for the work itself and, after something unusual or every few rounds when something has changed, for its own initiative among the acts open to it, with a note to its community if it has one. What a model says enters as a proposal of grade C, the body of an act the community's rules admit or refuse.
5. **A guard on spending.** The key is read from the person's settings folder (`models.json`, kept as `koinonia`), never the repository. Every call is reserved against a ledger in the same folder (`koinonia-budget.json`, stop at 45 of 50 dollars) at its largest cost and refused before it is made if it could pass the stop; its cost is counted from the tokens reported (`science.models.Anthropic` now keeps them as `usage`). Model agents ask a stand-in until the person switches Claude on; the checks never ask a model.
6. **Missions with real material.** The commons' mission is to check the urban-science catalogue of indexes (ADR 0128), drafted by a model and unchecked: declared rules of aggregation for rule agents, the entries' text for model agents; a stewards' declared standard of two checks of different origins (and, where declared, of different kinds); disagreements settled by a guild. What the society finds is a proposal for the catalogue's keeper and changes nothing in the catalogue.
7. **Circulation between communities, declared by a league.** When an agent does accepted work outside its home community, that is a service between communities. A league on the first league's charter declares by a compact how services are kept: gift, credit (a debt repaid in kind, set off, no service past a limit of net debt), transferable credit (a claim on a third passed on in payment), cleared credit (cycles of debts the league's record shows cleared by each creditor releasing its part), or provision (by need, read from the record, nothing owed). Envoys may propose another arrangement and vote; the council decides by majority counted by community.
8. **Case studies kept as archives.** `run_case.py` runs a case without the page and keeps its trajectory; `record.py` seals each run's rounds in a journal by archeion's rule, writes the lineage of every finding as W3C PROV, packs the run as a BagIt bag by archeion and validates it, and draws its figures as SVG. The record is `research/experiments/2026-11-26-koinonia-cases/`.
9. **A fault in the domain's preset, mended.** Running a league showed that the first league's charter let its council propose and vote but gave no position the power to enact an amendment; envoys may now `amend` (the domain's 63 checks pass).

## 3. Consequences

- The design's questions are asked of a living society, its answers read from sealed records. In the triangle of wants, bilateral credit stalls, a claim passed on or a cycle cleared unblocks it, and provision needs no debts; envoys by Claude, starting from credit, moved the league to cleared credit, transferable credit and provision in turn, and the debts went; starting from gift, they kept gift.
- Positions, mandates, recusal, counts by community and the chronicle's seals, all thesmos's, are exercised by agents rather than by worked examples.
- Not built: persons acting from the page (an adjudication, a vote); crossings with receipts between the communities' chronicles (the API does not yet expose them; a guild's head is recorded in the assessment that carries its finding); free riders and verification of declared capacity; more than one provider of models, so that judgements counted by provider can differ; a fourth community joining the league by accession.
- **Provisional:** the name, the colour (a wild rose with a lichen), the port, the limit of net debt (3), the stand-in.

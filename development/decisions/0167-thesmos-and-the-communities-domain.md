# 0167. thesmos and the Communities Domain

- **Status:** Accepted, provisionally; amended by [0173](0173-communities-of-every-order-registries-organisations-and-trajectories.md)
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Decided by:** the user, on the design record [A Society of Human and AI Communities](../ideas/2026-11-25-a-society-of-human-and-ai-communities.md): "new foundation for institutions"; "please make a communities domain, inside it may be a subdomain for our idea. Then begin implement the design, to see, whether really works."

## 1. Context

On 2026-11-25 the user asked for the design and implementation of a society of human and AI communities: heterogeneous communities with internal orders of their own, participants with unequal intelligence and computation, no centre, and a money-like instrument that may appear first and be dissolved later. The design record answered with a common ground of acts and records, constitutions as data, circulation tested function by function, and polycentric government, with an executable sketch. An independent reading for error found seventeen problems in the design the same day, one of them an error (Proposition 6 as first stated); the user then chose the placement.

## 2. Decision

- **A sixteenth foundation, thesmos** (θεσμός, what is laid down: an institution; the working name kept), on archeion, symploke, morphe, apodeixis, calx and, in its checks, kategoria. It holds parties and origins, mandates, acts, commitments with set-off and novation, units of count, constitutions as data with their admission, the chronicle and its fold, and the facets of a norm; the schema of the ground is stated as categories and answered by kategoria. Eight modules, 68 checks (its decisions 0001 to 0003).
- **A domain `communities`,** placed for now as a field at height 0 on the foundations alone; it becomes the bridge over law, economics, sociology, diplomacy and allocation that the design proposes when its claims, returns, federation and capacity modules first need them. Its modules `presets` (six constitutions as data, the first league's charter among them) and `circulation`.
- **A subdomain `communities/human-ai/`** for the society of human and AI communities: `lending` (a bounded contribution) and `jar` (circulation under scarcity, the user's hypothesis put to a construction).
- **Corrections from the reading for error** carried into the code: Proposition 6 restated; the slice per resource and enforced; charges only under the act's own mandate; units of count by person and by community; control a function; amendments re-admitted; conversions declared only by acts.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

- **Modules inside kybernesis.** Set aside at the user's choice.
- **The domain as a bridge at once.** Deferred: a domain declares what it uses, and its first modules use the foundations alone.
- **The work Koinonia now.** Deferred to the stages after the kernel and the jar (the implementation document, Section 8).

## 4. Consequences

- The general part of the diplomacy domain's `rules` may move to thesmos's procedures, in a change of its own.
- thesmos's repository is the user's to create; until then it lives in the working tree, unattached.
- The design record's text is corrected where the reading found errors, and the reading is kept with it.

# 0139. Economic Evaluators of Allocations Whose Order Matters

- **Status:** Accepted, provisionally
- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **Decided by:** the user, after ADR 0138: "yes, continuer, commit and push". The design's next stage is its Stage 7, the economic evaluators (`development/ideas/2026-11-20-evolutionary-allocation-of-heterogeneous-resources.md`), after the survey's Parts VI to VIII (`research/literature/reports/Evolutionary heterogeneous resource allocation.md`).

## 1. Context

The user asked that the fitness of an allocation could be "a economics value circulation simulation or even ABM, a LLM evaluator, a machine learning model". ADR 0137 and ADR 0138 built evaluators of closed forms, of a model's pairwise judgements and of people's; an allocation could not yet be evaluated by an economy in which money circulates, by a declared law of a distribution, or by laws learned from a record. The survey set out what each must be to be trusted: an agent economy whose accounts conserve and are checked every step, with payment timing, since without timing the order of grants cannot matter (Part VI); declared density laws whose consequences of a sequence of interventions are exact under their assumptions (Part VII); learned laws held to baselines and withheld outside the record's support, since order effects rest on the state-dependent terms a record identifies worst (Part VIII).

## 2. Decision

1. **The economics domain's `ledger`.** Balance sheets of instruments held against their issuers, kept on both sides by every agent; postings the only change, refused with their fault named (books that disagree, flows that disagree, money changed otherwise than by its issuer's lending, issuing, repaying or redeeming, a position turned negative), whole units of a declared minor unit so that every identity holds exactly; an outside node; the audit. An economy of agents acting period by period through nomos's behaviours, payments that cannot be met queued until money arrives, grants labelled with their origin; built from a social accounting matrix in its steady state, or as an economy of places whose households spend by whether they are short of money; Drăgulescu and Yakovenko's random exchange with its exact stationary law. Readings: payments, money traced from where it entered by rounds (retention, leakage, LM3 as the three-round truncation, the expected local holdings), the walk of money read from the payments, velocity and holding time, the circulating share by the domain's Hodge decomposition and the value within strongly connected components, concentration, sectoral net lending.
2. **nomos's behaviours of three origins** (its decision 0016): written rules, learned policies and a model's proposals behind one interface, each shown only what it declared it observes and refused an act it did not declare, a run tallied by origin and standing as the weakest origin that decided in it.
3. **kinesis's `densities`** (its decision 0009): densities on a grid of cells moved by a Markov generator whose drift and diffusion vary by cell, in switching types; the implicit flow keeping mass; the stationary density; interventions as maps on densities, composed in both orders for the exact effect of order under a law; a value equation whose policy is the drift, its generator the one the density's forward equation transposes. On calx's band LU (its decision 0020).
4. **kinesis's learned laws as evaluators** (its decision 0009): a law held to the naive forecasts by the test of equal accuracy (tyche's decision 0031), the record's support with steps beyond it named, an ensemble of laws giving the claim that one sequence is better a standing, bagged sparse laws with each term's inclusion probability.
5. **The allocation domain's `evaluators`:** an economy of places (each recipient a place, the grants reaching its households in the allocation's order, re-spending read from where each grant entered, the audit a verified constraint), a declared law of holdings with a mean field (grants filling the gap below a line), and an ensemble of learned laws (within the record's support, allocations ranked with a standing). The allocation domain now declares kinesis among its foundations.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

- **A static multiplier as the economic evaluator.** The domain's `incidence` has one; without payment timing and state-dependent spending the order of grants cannot matter, which the survey found is the point (Part VI §6.2).
- **Amounts in real numbers.** Conservation would hold only to rounding, and a leak could hide in it; whole units of a minor unit make every identity exact, and the readings, not the accounts, are real numbers.
- **The value equation and the agent economy in one domain module.** The value equation names no economy and is kinesis's; the economics domain may compose it with the ledger later.
- **Reference values from Achdou et al.'s codes.** MATLAB, not run; the checks use the permanent-income closed form and their propositions on the mass at the limit, and say so.

## 4. Consequences

- **Checks:** calx's band 17 (calx 1000); nomos's behaviours 16; tyche's forecasts 13 (tyche 1750); kinesis's densities 23 and evaluators 17 (kinesis 679); the economics domain's ledger 41; the allocation domain's evaluators 18 (118 in the domain).
- **What the checks show:** a traced grant's LM3 equals the closed form 1 + 0.8 + 0.8·0.6 and the walk read from the same payments to 10⁻⁸; random exchange reaches the exact law of a uniform configuration; in an economy of places the order of two grants changes what is re-spent by about 4% when households spend by whether they are short of money, and by 10⁻⁶ when they do not; a search over a fund's units driven by the economy keeps lawful, balanced allocations that grant the whole fund; under a law of holdings with a mean field the order changes the share below a line; a learned law that the fitted autoregression matches is not called better, and a ranking across learned laws turns undecided where a grant leaves the record's support.
- **Found while building:** a transfer between two banks' customers moves money between the banks' liabilities, so a transfer must keep the total money stock, not each issuer's; a grant shared equally among the few units below a line carries them past any grid, so the declared rule fills the gap below the line first; reflection at a grid's far end loses a little of a heavy tail, so the identities that hold exactly count the rate it removed.
- **Not built:** the API's composition and the Urban Studio's use of these evaluators; real stocks and revaluations in the ledger; calibration of habits to a record (tyche's `bayes`, simulation-based inference); a measurement layer and regime-switching laws in kinesis; Clark and West's and Giacomini and White's tests; surrogates of the agent economy with checked coverage (Stage 8).

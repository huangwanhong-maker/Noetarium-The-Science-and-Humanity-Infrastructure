# CLAUDE.md

This file guides Claude Code in the repository of Noetarium, the Science and Humanity Infrastructure ("the infrastructure"; The Science Infrastructures until 2026-11-06, [ADR 0077](development/decisions/0077-the-programme-named-noetarium.md)). It records the programme's purpose, design philosophy, architecture and working conventions.

This file can be revised: when a decision recorded here changes, update this file in the same change.

- The detailed design is in [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md).
- Decisions and the reasons for them are in [development/decisions/](development/decisions/). The present structure is [ADR 0026](development/decisions/0026-domains-as-libraries.md).
- Each foundation library under `foundations/` has its own CLAUDE.md, which governs work inside it.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This is an integrative programme to learn, use and research all domains of science, in theory and in practice, as one connected body of knowledge.

- **Three tiers** ([ADR 0026](development/decisions/0026-domains-as-libraries.md)):
  - **Foundations:** calx (computation and numerical methods), morphe (mathematical structures and their equivalences), physis (units, geometry, analysis, dynamics and theoretical physics), tyche (chance: probability, statistics and inference; [ADR 0048](development/decisions/0048-the-inference-foundation-tyche.md)) and nomos (the methods of the social sciences: measurement, index numbers, games, matching, social choice and econometrics, later surveys, networks and agent-based models; [ADR 0056](development/decisions/0056-nomos-and-a-toolbox-of-quantitative-economics.md)) and eikon (images and what is recovered from them: grids, scale space, tubular features, segmentation, distances, skeletons, surfaces, registration and fusion; [ADR 0070](development/decisions/0070-structures-reconstructed-from-images.md)) and heuresis (search over spaces too large to enumerate: evolutionary computation, evolution strategies, swarms, annealing, search distributions and their samplers, quantum search among them, many objectives, quality-diversity, programs, islands and surrogates, typed spaces with declared units and laws composed by products and choices, and orders of grants evaluated once per trace (its decisions 0008–0009); [ADR 0073](development/decisions/0073-the-search-foundation-heuresis.md)) and peira (designs and the mechanisms by which units come to be treated and observed, and what they identify: experiments and randomisation, weighting, surveys, survival and cohorts, missing data, quasi-experiments and causal graphs, interference (treatments that reach beyond their units), later meta-analysis and sensitivity; [ADR 0082](development/decisions/0082-peira-designs-and-the-mechanisms-of-observation.md)) and syntaxis (grammars whose words are geometric: geometric graph grammars learned by compression, made probabilistic and context-sensitive, and parsed; grammars whose words are 2-simplices; later hyperedge replacement and 3-complexes; [ADR 0083](development/decisions/0083-syntaxis-grammars-whose-words-are-geometric.md)) and kategoria (categories computed with: finite categories and those presented by generators and equations, functors, adjunctions, limits, set-valued functors and monoidal theories, resource types with their laws verified and each verdict scoped, allocations as morphisms and the order of grants decided on finite systems (its decision 0004), every claim answered with its evidence, and a language in which such claims are stated; [ADR 0100](development/decisions/0100-kategoria-and-the-category-studio.md)) and kinesis (dynamical systems as objects of study and of inference: a language in which a system is declared by its change, structure, constraint and difference, whatever its kind (a flow, a map, a system with events, a stochastic differential, a system given by its action, an open quantum system, a rewriting system, a system made of systems); what a declared law does over time; what a record identifies, braids of trajectories first, and the paths a record of positions admits under a bound on speed (Hägerstrand's prisms, [ADR 0127](development/decisions/0127-theories-of-the-case-in-space-and-time.md)); and laws recovered from records, and balance laws of a density on a graph of cells, run and learned from a few snapshots; and critical transitions read from records of structured states, each detector saying what it can see and is blind to, how often its test is wrong at the record's length, and a verdict of signal, no signal or undecidable ([ADR 0135](development/decisions/0135-detectors-of-critical-transitions.md)); densities on a grid of cells moved by a generator whose drift and diffusion vary by cell, a value equation whose policy drives them, and laws learned from a record held to baselines, withheld outside its support and read across an ensemble ([ADR 0139](development/decisions/0139-economic-evaluators-of-allocations.md)); [ADR 0103](development/decisions/0103-kinesis-and-the-dynamics-lab.md), [ADR 0124](development/decisions/0124-population-dynamics.md)) and apodeixis (logic and verification: formulas and sequents, calculi declared as rule schemata whose derivations a small checker verifies, decision procedures with countermodels, satisfiability with checked refutations, argumentation, and derivability relative to an interpretation, Γ ⊢_ι φ; [ADR 0112](development/decisions/0112-apodeixis-and-tekmerion.md)) and tekmerion (evidence and its interpretation: charts of evidence read as argument and as probability, the force of a report through its source, networks and belief functions, the reconstruction of what happened (accounts in space and time with every person's path checked at their speed, searched for by evolution, kept as the best account of each kind and tested on what was held back, [ADR 0127](development/decisions/0127-theories-of-the-case-in-space-and-time.md)), the recognition of evidence, interpreters that read a norm by any method (a law, bounds, precedents, a regression, a decision over purposes, a POMDP over a sequence of cases, a model's proposal) composed with what follows and joined in panels, and the warrant of evidence, reports that share an origin counted once, evidence located at a place and a time, and findings construed: read under every reading of their open terms, each reading with its source, with their pivots, rivals and standing; [ADR 0112](development/decisions/0112-apodeixis-and-tekmerion.md), [ADR 0114](development/decisions/0114-evidence-based-policy-and-interpretation-by-models.md), [ADR 0123](development/decisions/0123-evidence-in-space-and-time.md), [ADR 0126](development/decisions/0126-needs-read-under-their-terms-and-a-model-in-every-part.md)) and archeion (archives: holdings and their fixity, with proofs of inclusion and of consistency, sameness under a declared criterion, arrangement by provenance and original order, the provenance of what was made from what, chains of custody, retention and access by declared rules, packages for transfer; [ADR 0113](development/decisions/0113-archeion-and-the-law-domain.md)) and zetesis (case-oriented inquiry, the traditional analysis of the social sciences and the humanities: cases as members of crisp and fuzzy sets, qualitative comparative analysis with every minimal model, the coding of qualitative data, concepts and typologies, the selection of cases, process tracing; [ADR 0117](development/decisions/0117-zetesis-case-oriented-inquiry.md)) and symploke (linked data: RDF's terms and documents, N-Triples, N-Quads, Turtle, TriG and JSON-LD; a store whose edits undo; SPARQL's core; entailment by RDFS and part of OWL 2 RL with derivations; SHACL Core; graphs compared up to their blank nodes; [ADR 0118](development/decisions/0118-symploke-linked-data.md)) and kybernesis (steering a system known through evidence: the architecture of the loop from evidence to action declared as data, revised and admitted when its constraints hold and kategoria answers its categorical claims; methods, formal and statistical, admitted to its blocks when kategoria decides their laws; dual verdicts; the loop run; [ADR 0130](development/decisions/0130-kybernesis-steering-by-evidence.md); the record of a steering, every value with how it came to be (a method, an observation, a person's entry, an amendment, a review), with files, branches and lineage, sealed in archeion's journal, and practices carried between relational systems by a lens with their support factors, [ADR 0131](development/decisions/0131-an-engagement-with-a-city.md); a block's method chosen among its alternatives by a sealed act, and the ontology of facts declared as data, revised with a mapping that kategoria decides is a functor of its kinds, records carried along it (its decision 0005)). They hold laws and methods that name no object in nature. Their mathematics is mapped, area by area against the whole Mathematics Subject Classification, in [docs/mathematics.md](docs/mathematics.md) ([ADR 0035](development/decisions/0035-the-mathematical-foundations.md)); a module is written when a domain needs it.
  - **Domains:** one library per science, holding its objects and their models. A domain is a field or a bridge between fields.
  - **Works:** applications, research and notebooks, organised by purpose, each composing the domains it needs.
- **Shared edges** touch the world for every work: data, visualization, observation, and the API through which other programs reach the infrastructure (ADR 0030).
- **The first domains** are those of the Earth and the sky:
  - **Fields:** metrology, geodesy, astronomy, geophysics, geography, oceanography and meteorology.
  - **Bridges:** tides, navigation, air–sea exchange and climate.
- **Scope of the first fields:**
  - **Geography** covers all its branches: physical geography, cartography and GIS, remote sensing, human geography, and others as the work requires.
  - **Meteorology** is atmospheric science in the broad sense; ocean and atmosphere are one coupled system ([ADR 0007](development/decisions/0007-atmosphere-and-ocean-as-coupled-system.md)).
  - **Geodesy** supports the other Earth sciences.
  - **Geophysics** is the solid Earth: plate motion, earthquakes and the crust ([ADR 0024](development/decisions/0024-the-solid-earth.md)), and the Earth beneath a place to its centre, CRUST1.0 over PREM ([ADR 0055](development/decisions/0055-the-earth-beneath.md)).
- **Neuroscience** is the first domain beyond the Earth and the sky ([ADR 0028](development/decisions/0028-neuroscience-and-the-brain-atlas.md)): the brain's reference spaces, parcellations and maps, and the head with its electrodes, drawn by the Brain Atlas. It is a repository of its own, attached as a submodule.
- **History** is the first domain of the humanities ([ADR 0041](development/decisions/0041-the-history-domain.md)), built at the user's request for the History Complex. It covers records of histories coded by a codebook, their chronology across calendars, the 2-complex a record makes, and the spin foam of its signed ties, computed exactly on physis; and the history read as a field theory, one Feynman graph of a group field theory and, in time, one sample path ([ADR 0042](development/decisions/0042-the-history-as-a-field-theory.md)).
- **Historical objects** ([ADR 0110](development/decisions/0110-historical-objects-and-histos-harmonia.md)), at the user's request for a history archive and learning infrastructure, worked with in Histos Harmonia. A historical object is anything in history that can be inquired into (an event, a period, a person, a polity, a work, a theory, a kind of music, a problem), of a kind in an order of kinds beneath one root; the user's "extends" is three relations kept apart (a kind is a kind of another; an object specialises an object; an object exemplifies a classifying object), and facts are never inherited. The history domain's `objects` holds records of them (`noetarium.histos/1`, its codebook of objects): relations typed by the kinds of their ends, and emergences, the appearing of an object with its premises together, each in a role (the problem answered, a condition, a source, what is argued against). Historical materialism is kept as the conditions always recorded and the four questions always asked (what problem it answers, why that arose, what made it answerable, to whom it mattered and what it made possible), never as a thesis asserted. Every statement carries who says it, with a grade (A a structured source, B a text with its passage, C a language model from memory), and stands as established, attested, proposed or disputed by a stated rule; a reader takes a layer, the default the established alone, so that a model's word is never read as the record's. Its `dependence` reads the order: cones, the frontier of problems at a date, what rests on an object, comparison cases, the four questions with the record's silences said. The schema is stated as categories and verified by kategoria, which the domain now declares; the general order theory went to morphe (its decision 0015).
- **Musicology and philosophy** are founded with code ([ADR 0111](development/decisions/0111-musicology-and-philosophy-founded.md)), for the same request ("only consider philosophy history and music history" first), each a field at height 0 on calx and morphe, C++ from the start. Musicology: `pitch` (intervals as exact ratios, tempered ones as primes to rational exponents, so that "the circle closes" is an equality) and `tuning` (Pythagorean, sanfen sunyi, just, meantone, equal, well temperaments, al-Urmawī's seventeen steps, each measured against the pure intervals; 145 checks). Philosophy: `arguments` (argument maps after IBIS, evaluated under the grounded semantics of Dung's frameworks, which moved to apodeixis by [ADR 0112](development/decisions/0112-apodeixis-and-tekmerion.md) with 53 of the domain's checks; 37 here). Neither names a history: what appeared when, and why, is the history domain's record.
- **Genomics and evolution** are the first domains of the life sciences ([ADR 0043](development/decisions/0043-the-genome-infrastructure.md)), built at the user's request for genome analysis and worked with in the Genome Workbench ([ADR 0045](development/decisions/0045-the-genome-workbench.md)). Genomics (height 0) holds genomes and their sequences: the alignment of sequences as a sum over an edit graph in a semiring with its fast schedules, indexes and sketches, k-mers and assembly, mapping and variants, HMMs and annotation, multiple alignment, expression, and simulation with its truth, and, from the survey of advanced genome analysis ([ADR 0047](development/decisions/0047-group-grammars-and-advanced-genome-analysis.md)), RNA structure by stochastic grammars, spectra, taxonomic classification, whole-genome comparison and group grammars of nucleic acids, the user's GE-CSG draft made exact; evolution (height 1, on genomics) holds phylogeny, population genetics, selection and haplotypes. From [ADR 0064](development/decisions/0064-abnormal-genomes-their-description-and-expression.md), at the user's request for abnormal genomes, "an international representation description system" and expression prediction: genomics describes variants by HGVS (`nomenclature`) and chromosomes by ISCN (`karyotype`), each a grammar parsed by morphe's BNF reader and given its meaning by computation; detects structural variants, copy number and losses of heterozygosity from reads (`structural`) and a tumour's somatic genome (`somatic`); and reads expression from sequence (`codon`, `regulation`); evolution's `association` goes from genotype to trait and to expression. Their general mathematics is in the foundations: words, their indexes and their alignment, grammars and their parsers in morphe's `formal`; group actions, characters and group algebras in morphe's `algebra`; vector lanes, task graphs, the FFT and the statistics they need in calx; change points, penalised regression, factorisations, fine-mapping, variance components, multiple testing and networks in tyche.
- **The domains of life** are placed in the order beside neuroscience, genomics and evolution, all planned ([ADR 0061](development/decisions/0061-the-domains-of-life.md)), at the user's request: molecular science (height 0, with structural biology and biochemistry); cell biology on genomics and molecular science; microbiology on genomics, evolution and cell biology; physiology on cell biology and neuroscience; developmental biology on cell biology; and ecology on geography, climate and evolution (height 5, the highest in the order). The record says what each holds against its neighbours (§2.3). Their general methods go to the foundations when a module first needs them. **Ecology** has since been founded with code ([ADR 0101](development/decisions/0101-ecology-founded-the-urban-forest.md)), at the user's request for "the ecosystem, biosystem, and life analysis methods" of cities, with a handbook of methods and tools: C++ from the start, its first object a forest of individual trees (`taxonomy`, `biodiversity`, `populations`, `forest`, `connectivity`, and since [ADR 0109](development/decisions/0109-ecosystem-services.md) `ecosystems`, InVEST's four services on a grid of land cover; 144 checks), its general methods first in calx (non-negative matrices), tyche (classes and communities), peira (imperfect detection) and physis (circuits). Urban questions compose it with urban science through the API, since it stands above it in the order.
- **Planetary science** is the domain of the planets and moons as worlds ([ADR 0052](development/decisions/0052-fields-known-in-part.md)), founded at the user's request ahead of its place in tier 3, for the Moon's magnetic field in Cosmopoiesis. It is a bridge over astronomy, geophysics and meteorology, and its first module, magnetism, holds the Moon's crustal field (T2015_449, the law of its spectrum and the part it leaves unresolved) and the interplanetary field carried to it from L1; its second, surfaces, the Moon's ground as a robot's foot meets it, presumed and realised ([ADR 0059](development/decisions/0059-robots-in-their-environments.md)). Its general mathematics is in the foundations: potential fields, random fields and stationary random fields in physis's `analysis`, the Legendre functions in calx.
- **Anatomy** is the domain of the structures of the body ([ADR 0066](development/decisions/0066-the-anatomy-domain-and-the-body-atlas.md)), founded at the user's request with the Body Atlas, ahead of its place in tier 4. It is a field at height 0 on the foundations alone, C++ from the start:
  - the anatomical frames: LPS of DICOM and BodyParts3D, RAS of NIfTI, the radiological and neurological displays;
  - FMA's concepts in their is-a and part-of trees, and the seventeen systems of the body by an ordered table;
  - BodyParts3D's adult man, read from the data library; and the Human Reference Atlas's woman (CC BY 4.0), her systems by the Atlas's layers ([ADR 0072](development/decisions/0072-a-womans-body.md));
  - each part's topology, solid, principal axes and extents, compounds, and the parts that touch;
  - the body cut in the planes of imaging, at its vertebral levels;
  - a seed knowledge base of fifty entries.

  Its general mathematics went to physis (triangle surfaces, their sections and the solids they bound; decision 0019) and morphe (surfaces given as triangles; decision 0010). Its module `vessels` holds vascular trees, rooted, measured and named, with Murray's exponent fitted ([ADR 0070](development/decisions/0070-structures-reconstructed-from-images.md)).
- **Medical imaging** is the domain of images of the body and the structures reconstructed from them ([ADR 0070](development/decisions/0070-structures-reconstructed-from-images.md)), founded at the user's request ("medical-imaging only is ok") for Fabrica, a field at height 1 on anatomy. It holds only what is medical: DICOM series in the patient frame (`dicom`), the modalities' physics and forward models (`modalities`: CT, TOF-MRA), vessels reconstructed from one image (`reconstruction`: detected by vesselness, delineated at the local half-maximum, thinned to anatomy's vascular tree, bounded by a surface) and from several together (`integration`: registered, fused, arteries told from veins). Every method on images is eikon's. Veins first; the flow in them is the next stage, on physis's `fluids` (its decision 0021) and physiology's `circulation`, founded with anatomy beneath it.
- **Robotics** is the domain of systems that sense and act ([ADR 0054](development/decisions/0054-bodies-dynamics-and-knowledge.md)), founded at the user's word ("you may add a domain robotics, if needed") ahead of its place in tier 4, for the rover of Cosmopoiesis. It is a field at height 0 on the foundations alone: vehicles on a sphere at two fidelities (kinematic and dynamic, translated exactly), following waypoints, survey plans and sensors' mounts; and a body with joints, a hopping rabbit for the Moon ([ADR 0058](development/decisions/0058-a-body-with-joints.md)), its controller and three methods of its motion; a fish in the Earth's ocean ([ADR 0059](development/decisions/0059-robots-in-their-environments.md)), swimming by a travelling wave in water modelled strip by strip; and a dog trotting on the Earth's ground, its four legs on diagonal pairs. A robot is a body with a controller: its mechanics is physis's (motion from a discrete action, rigid bodies, planar trees, compliant contact), what names a vehicle, its wheels, its motors and its ground the domain's.
- **Fields** are one model for every science ([ADR 0053](development/decisions/0053-fields-and-their-visual-expression.md)): a field is a section over its base, with its fibre, representation and provenance (physis's `fields`). A domain presents its models as fields. A law that advances a field (a balance law, a jump, a reaction network) is separate from the field and from the time integrator. A drawing of a field is a visual expression of the shared scene model.
- **Urban science** is the domain of streets and buildings as open systems ([ADR 0060](development/decisions/0060-streets-as-open-systems.md)), founded at the user's request with the Urban Science Workbench, a 3D GIS of cities (Melbourne's centre; Lower Manhattan from Valdrada's compiled city, [ADR 0065](development/decisions/0065-new-york-in-the-workbench.md)) whose first plug-in, *Street Oceanography*, makes the user's papers exact (the user's "street oceanography lab"); the papers' apparatus is in the domain's theory note, not in the page. A street is terrain, flows, carried properties and record, mutually altering; the domain computes what the papers carry, proves what holds of any street, and draws mechanisms (fluid, chemical, thermodynamic) only as representations offered for exclusion, as the papers withhold dynamical laws. It is the bridge over architecture, at height 1: it declares architecture so that a city's buildings and streets are one network of volumes and interfaces, whose events its module `events` makes a 2-complex, the *Event Spacetime* of the workbench ([ADR 0067](development/decisions/0067-event-spacetime.md)). Its general methods went to calx (multitaper spectra, bispectra), tyche (harmonic analysis, Lomb–Scargle, red-noise nulls, co-location) and physis (kinematic waves, master equations and entropy production, mixing, reaction–diffusion). Its module `landscape` holds districts as cellular configurations whose events rewrite them, structure, use and meaning in one process, drawn as the district's spacetime by the workbench's second plug-in, *Landscape Dynamics* ([ADR 0062](development/decisions/0062-landscapes-as-cellular-configurations.md)); its general methods went to morphe (regular complexes, their rewriting, traces, Möbius inversion) and physis (composition of open systems, lumpability, piecewise-deterministic processes, elimination, Doi–Peliti operators, Hodge Laplacians). Architecture is a domain of its own; human flow is begun by the module `movement` (walkers as agents, ADR 0075), and culture is planned as urban science's module. Its module `luti` feeds land use and transport back year by year ([ADR 0094](development/decisions/0094-land-use-and-transport-interacting.md)): dwellings after rents, households choosing by `housing`'s market with each place valued for its access to jobs, and the journeys to work in Evans's equilibrium on congested roads. Its module `section` reads a street as what is measured between the faces that stand on it ([ADR 0098](development/decisions/0098-the-street-a-building-stands-on.md)): the width, the enclosure and the sky along its length, each side's line, the strips it is divided into with what a scheme asks found one by one, the sun on its footpaths, and how high each building beside it may rise. Its `indicators` are the indexes of cities ([ADR 0128](development/decisions/0128-indexes-and-cities-across-scales.md)): a catalogue of 1,536 indexes, quantitative and qualitative, drawn from a keyword atlas the user gave, each with how it is carried up a hierarchy of units (summed, a weighted mean, a ratio of sums, recomputed, at one scale only), how it is observed and estimated, its role in a dynamical model and where the infrastructure computes it, every keyword of the atlas accounted for; data, drafted by a language model and unchecked.
- **Architecture** is the domain of buildings: their form, the spaces they enclose and join (plans and districts as cell complexes), the frontage at which a building meets its street, the rules that bound them, and later their environment (ADR 0060 §2.8). It was founded as a domain of its own at the user's word ("Only architecture independent in domain"), not as a module of urban science. Urban science keeps the street and the city, and is their bridge: it declares architecture (ADR 0067), and may later declare geography or economics. The city composed for a purpose is the Urban Science Workbench's. It stands at height 0 on the foundations; its first modules, `space` (a building as a complex of rooms and its events; a district's buildings as one 3-complex, each building a 3-cell, shared walls shared 2-cells), `environment` (their heat as a Hodge Laplacian under the Sun; since [ADR 0089](development/decisions/0089-what-could-be-built.md) sun and shade on streets and faces, and a day's balance of an envelope) and `syntax` (footprints and outlines read by syntaxis's grammars, ADR 0083), are written (ADR 0062, [ADR 0063](development/decisions/0063-the-district-as-a-complex-to-work-on.md)), and so, for the generative design loop (ADR 0089), are `rules` (the envelope a lot's rules allow, and compliance), `form` (a massing within it) and `frontage` (the street wall). Since [ADR 0095](development/decisions/0095-the-design-studio.md), at the user's request for the design of a single building before the street, it also holds a building as a person draws it: `plan` (walls on a plane, storey by storey; the rooms as the faces, their net areas and widths, light and air, the access graph; a code of habitation as declared limits; quantities, cost as a range and the fabric's heat loss), `programme` (a brief as a labelled graph, answered statement by statement) and, in `rules`, the siting of a dwelling on its lot, rule by rule. No limit in it is any jurisdiction's until a scheme states it. Since [ADR 0096](development/decisions/0096-what-stands-in-the-rooms.md), at the user's request to go into a design at eye height and furnish it, its `interior` holds what stands in the rooms: pieces with the clear floor they need, each found within its room, apart from the others, usable, and reached by a person of a width, on eikon's distance transform, which the domain now declares. Since [ADR 0097](development/decisions/0097-the-shell-of-a-drawn-building.md), at the user's word to go on to the outside, its `shell` holds a drawn building's outside (the footprint at the walls' outer faces, a roof flat, shed, gabled or hipped on physis's roof of equal slope, the walls carried up to it, the faces to the eight winds), and `environment` the sun, the sky and the average daylight factor at its glass (`panes`) and how high a building may rise before it takes the sun from where it is wanted (`solar`, [ADR 0098](development/decisions/0098-the-street-a-building-stands-on.md)).
- **Economics** is the first domain of the social sciences beside history ([ADR 0044](development/decisions/0044-value-in-space-and-time.md)), built at the user's request for the circulation of value in space and in time and drawn by the Economy Atlas. It holds value carried between positions: transports and their loop deficits (prices exactly when every deficit vanishes), the circulation of flows on a network whose faces geography supplies, production as a circular flow, and the spacetime complex of a record of transactions; and, for the Economy Atlas's toolbox of quantitative economics ([ADR 0056](development/decisions/0056-nomos-and-a-toolbox-of-quantitative-economics.md)), growth (Solow, growth accounting), the short run (IS–LM) and trade (its partners and its gravity); and, for the atlas's Decisions view ([ADR 0074](development/decisions/0074-perception-and-decision-in-the-economy-atlas.md)), the regimes of the business cycle read from growth (`regimes`) and decisions taken on that belief (`policy`); and, as an evaluator of allocations whose order matters ([ADR 0139](development/decisions/0139-economic-evaluators-of-allocations.md)), an economy's accounts and agents (`ledger`): balance sheets kept on both sides, postings the only change and refused when the books, the flows or money disagree, an outside node, agents acting through nomos's behaviours with payments queued until money arrives, and money traced from where it entered (retention, LM3, the walk of money, velocity, the circulating share), and since [ADR 0141](development/decisions/0141-evolutionary-allocation-on-real-problems-and-records.md) its habits calibrated to records, the accounts first, then behaviour; and since [ADR 0145](development/decisions/0145-the-finances-of-local-governments.md) `fiscal`, the finances of a local or regional government: accounts through a declared chart, ratios against a declared scheme of thresholds, the years ahead with its loans, the largest new loan keeping the coverage of debt service at a declared floor, debt dynamics with a fan, capacity and effort; and since [ADR 0146](development/decisions/0146-land-value-capture-and-readjustment.md) `capture`, land value capture and land readjustment: uplift as declared values with and without a project, a levy, development rights, tax increment and a developer obligation as streams of payments with their payers, cost recovered and uplift taken kept apart, parcels charged twice over named, a readjustment's area identity and contribution ratio, and replotting by assignment with settlements summing to zero. Its general mathematics is in the foundations: discrete exterior calculus and points on the sphere in physis, conjugate gradients in calx, the worldline complex in morphe, regression in tyche, and the measures and econometrics of the social sciences in nomos.
- **Physical computing** is the domain of computation by the dynamics of matter ([ADR 0076](development/decisions/0076-physical-computing.md)), founded as planned at the user's request ("emerging computation tech worth a domain"; "utilize the full list"). It holds substrates as computers: a square that commutes within a tolerance, with an encoding and a readout of a class fixed in advance. They come in fourteen families, from analogue electronics, reaction networks and DNA (the broadest, where breadth is a theorem) to water in rotating tanks and ecological microcosms (the deepest), quantum reservoirs and robot swarms. One infrastructure makes these connections explicit, and lets one tested implementation of each method serve every science.

## 2. Design Philosophy

The infrastructure will grow over a long time and will change direction as new ideas emerge. Its governing principle is **modular, extensible, revisable**.

### 2.1 Modularity

- **Knowledge by subject, work by purpose.** Domains hold knowledge; works compose it. Works cover the domains; no work owns a domain.
- **An order of domains.** Each domain declares in `module.json` the domains and foundations it may use. The declared relation is acyclic, so that its closure is a partial order, and a domain's library is its down-set.
- **Public interfaces.** Units use each other only through their public interfaces: today `js/index.js`, later the C++ headers.
- **Reuse before writing.** A domain uses what the foundations already hold and never writes it again; a general method a domain needs (one that names no object in nature) is written in the foundation where it belongs, and the domain then uses it. A reusable, integrative foundation is the purpose of the repository (the user's principle, [ADR 0042](development/decisions/0042-the-history-as-a-field-theory.md)).
- **Independence is checked.** The rules are verified by `development/tools/check-dependencies.mjs`, and each domain's checks run alone with its down-set by `development/tools/check-alone.mjs`.

### 2.2 Extensibility

- A new science is a new domain; a new method, device or presentation technique is a new module. Adding one does not mean editing existing ones.
- Computing devices and presentation media keep changing. In both places, a stable, device-neutral representation separates *what* from *how*, and each technology is a pluggable backend (§3.5, §3.6).
- Own the interfaces and borrow the implementations: mature external libraries may sit behind the infrastructure's interfaces.
- Prefer small, composable units. Introduce an abstraction only after its pattern has appeared twice.

### 2.3 Revisability

- Every module, note and model carries a status:
  - `planned`: named in the architecture, with no content yet;
  - `seed`: an idea, possibly wrong;
  - `draft`: being worked out;
  - `stable`: tested and relied upon;
  - `deprecated`: superseded, and kept with a pointer to its replacement.
- Significant decisions go into the decision log (§6.3).
- Prefer reversible choices, and mark provisional ones where they are made.
- Capture ideas early (§6.2) and promote them once they mature.

### 2.4 Unity of Theory and Computation

- Every implemented formula cites its derivation or source, with equation numbers.
- Every theoretical result that can be checked numerically should be checked by code.
- Notation in the notes corresponds to names in the code, and the glossary maps one to the other.
- A domain's notes live beside its code, in its `theory/`.

### 2.5 Verifiability

- Numerical code is validated against authoritative references, such as IAU SOFA/ERFA, JPL Horizons, the IERS Conventions and NOAA tide predictions.
- Reference values live in the tests of the domain they verify (`tests/fixtures/`), each with its source and retrieval date.
- Docstrings state limits of accuracy: model error, range of validity, and approximations made.

### 3.3 Pure Core, Effectful Edges

The foundations and the domains perform pure computation:

- no network access;
- no instrument I/O;
- no user interface;
- no hidden global state;
- only files whose paths are passed in are read.

The edges (`data/`, `observation/`, `visualization/`, `api/`) and the works (`applications/`, `research/`, `notebooks/`) deal with the outside world and call the core.

### 3.4 Dependency Rules

- calx and morphe are independent of each other; physis depends on both. No foundation depends on a domain, an edge or a work.
- A domain depends only on what its `module.json` declares, through public interfaces.
- Nothing in `foundations/` or `domains/` depends on an edge or a work.
- `applications/` may depend on everything below it, and nothing depends on an application.
- Only `applications/`, `notebooks/` and `research/` may use `visualization/`.
- A domain's tests import only what the domain may import, the harness (`development/tools/checks.mjs`) and Node's built-in modules. A check that joins domains which do not stand on each other belongs to a work.
- An upward dependency is a design error. Fix it by moving code down, or by dependency inversion.

### 3.5 Computation Stack (calx)

- calx separates representation, schedule, compiler, runtime, task scheduler and backends, following TVM, Halide and MLIR.
- A new device is a new backend directory, with no other change.
- The CPU reference interpreter defines correct results.
- Precision is declared for each computation and never silently reduced.
- The domains never address a device directly.

## 4. Scientific and Mathematical Conventions

### 4.1 Units, Time Scales and Reference Frames

These are the most common source of silent error in this subject, so they are always explicit.

- **Units.** Use SI units internally. Use radians for angles internally, and convert only at input and output boundaries. When a quantity's unit is not obvious, carry it in the name or the type.
- **Time.** A time value always states its scale (UTC, TAI, TT, TDB or UT1) and its representation (JD, MJD, two-part JD or ISO 8601). Epochs are named, for example J2000.0 = JD 2451545.0 TT.
- **Reference frames.**
  - Every coordinate states its frame, and where relevant its epoch and equinox.
  - Examples of frames: ICRS, GCRS, CIRS, ITRS, ecliptic of date, topocentric, local horizon.
  - Transformations between frames are explicit functions and never happen implicitly.
- **Figure of the Earth.** State the ellipsoid or geoid in use, such as WGS 84 or GRS 80.
- **Names of places** ([ADR 0069](development/decisions/0069-names-of-places-and-a-2d-gis.md), at the user's request). Every product and map names places by the data library's policy (`data/products/naming.json`):
  - names are Natural Earth's long names, except where a name is in dispute or out of date; those take the UN's M49 short name, or else the usage of the WTO, the OECD, the World Bank and the IMF (Chinese Taipei, not Taiwan, for the economy);
  - a map draws no name for a territory whose status is in dispute, nor for a sea whose name is in dispute;
  - areas in dispute are drawn in no claimant's colour and unnamed, and a place in one has no country;
  - prose follows the same usage, while geographic names (the island of Taiwan, the Taiwan Strait) stay.
- **Orientation conventions.** Define each of these once in the glossary and follow it everywhere:
  - handedness;
  - the origin of azimuth;
  - the sign of longitude;
  - whether rotations are active or passive.

Domains of other sciences add their own conventions in their READMEs, such as the nomenclature of chemistry.

### 4.2 Notation, Rigour and Glossary

- Write notes in LaTeX, with the precision of a mathematics text:
  - state the hypotheses;
  - distinguish definitions from results;
  - give proofs or cite them.
- Where it is natural, show algebraic structure rather than hiding it: groups, representations, lattices, characters and complexes.
- `docs/glossary.md` lists every symbol with its meaning, units, convention and code name. It is created in Phase 0.

### 4.3 References

Cite primary sources and standard references, and collect them in `docs/references.bib`. Examples:

- the *Explanatory Supplement to the Astronomical Almanac*;
- the IERS Conventions and IAU resolutions;
- Meeus, *Astronomical Algorithms*;
- Pugh and Woodworth, *Sea-Level Science*;
- Gill, *Atmosphere–Ocean Dynamics*;
- Vallis, *Atmospheric and Oceanic Fluid Dynamics*;
- Holton and Hakim, *An Introduction to Dynamic Meteorology*;
- Aki and Richards, *Quantitative Seismology*.

### 4.4 Writing Style

These conventions are shared with the foundation libraries.

- Headings are declarative, numbered and academic, for example "3.2 Precession of the Equinoxes". Never use question forms.
- Present concepts and philosophy before structure and usage.
- Label asides with *Rationale.*, *Remark.* or *Caveat.*
- Code identifiers, docstrings and comments are in English.

## 6. Working Procedures

### 6.1 The Contract of a Domain and a Module

A domain directory contains:

- `README.md`: purpose, status (§2.3), scope by module, dependencies, references and open questions;
- `module.json`: `name`, `kind` (`field` or `bridge`), `status`, `depends` and `foundations`;
- `js/`: provisional JavaScript, behind `js/index.js`, until the C++ exists;
- `tests/`: its checks (`run-tests.mjs`, on the shared harness), with reference values in `tests/fixtures/`;
- `theory/`: its notes;
- later, its C++ modules, which follow [architecture §6](docs/architecture.md).

### 6.2 Idea Capture

- Write each new idea in `development/ideas/` as `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`, with status `seed`, and add it to the index.
- An idea that concerns only one foundation goes in that library's own `development/ideas/`.
- Promote an idea by moving its substance and leaving a link behind.
- Never delete an abandoned idea. Mark it `abandoned` and give one sentence explaining why.

### 6.3 Decision Log

- Record decisions that shape structure, conventions or tooling in `development/decisions/NNNN-slug.md`, using the template in the index.
- Never rewrite a record. A new record supersedes or amends it, and the old record's status is updated to point to the new one. Paths in old records are as they were written; ADR 0026 maps them to the present.
- Records accepted so far: 0001–0148. Records 0001–0024 were written in the first laboratory, which the infrastructure absorbed; 0025 founded this repository.

### 6.4 Release Notes

- At each commit, add to `development/release-notes/YYYY-MM-DD.md` of each repository committed (one file per day, one short section per commit, newest first) a clear, concise summary in plain prose of what is new, what one can now do and what works better, and index the day in that folder's README ([ADR 0037](development/decisions/0037-release-notes.md)).
- Not a list of changed files or functions: the git log keeps those.
- Not between commits.

### 6.5 Change Discipline

- Before restructuring directories, adding or renaming a domain, moving a foundation, or changing a convention in this file, propose the change and wait for the user's agreement.
- Keep each change to one concept.
- When adding a domain or module, update:
  - its README and `module.json`;
  - its parent README's table;
  - the layout (§5), if it adds a directory here;
  - the glossary, if it introduces notation.
- If this file no longer matches the repository, say so and propose a fix.

### 6.6 Checks

- `node development/tools/run-tests.mjs` checks the dependency rules, runs every suite (the foundations' C++, built with CMake here or in WSL; the foundations' JavaScript; the domains in their order, each with its C++; visualization, the data library's Python checks in `data/tests/`, the API, applications), then runs each domain alone with its down-set. Run it before every commit that touches code.
- C++ checks are programs on calx's `verification`, registered with CTest; `development/tools/checks.mjs` builds a CMake project and counts them (`cppChecks`), keeping its builds in `~/.cache/science-build/`. One library's C++ alone: `node development/tools/run-cpp.mjs foundations/calx foundations/calx`.
- A domain's own suite: `node domains/<name>/tests/run-tests.mjs`.
- Check the applications visually by driving headless Chrome over the DevTools protocol, with an absolute `--user-data-dir`.

### 6.7 Working with the Foundations

- **Submodule commits.** Edit a foundation in place under `foundations/<name>` and commit there first. Then commit the updated submodule pointer here. Commit only the pointer, by path, when other changes are staged. The same holds for the neuroscience domain under `domains/neuroscience` and for Cosmopoiesis under `applications/cosmopoiesis-lab`.
- **Commit order.** calx and morphe first, then physis, then tyche, then peira, then nomos, then eikon, then heuresis, then syntaxis, then kategoria, then kinesis, then apodeixis, then archeion, then tekmerion, then zetesis, then symploke, then kybernesis, then neuroscience, then Cosmopoiesis, then this repository.
- **Each foundation's own CLAUDE.md** governs work inside it.
- **Links between repositories.** Use absolute GitHub URLs.
- **Shared conventions.** The writing style, status vocabulary, and decision, idea and release-note conventions are shared by every repository. Change them in all of them together.
- **Pushing.** Push only with the user's agreement.

### 6.8 Collaboration Style

- The principal investigator is fluent in abstract algebra and formal language theory. Prefer precise mathematical formulations.
- This is also a learning project. When introducing physics or conventions specific to a domain, give the reasoning behind them and cite their source.
- Visualizations should look warm and humane, not like a generic dashboard.

  - **Imagery and relief.** Satellite imagery or relief forms the base ([ADR 0018](development/decisions/0018-fine-relief-at-close-range.md)).
  - **Climate:** Köppen–Geiger classes from 1901 to 2099, normals, climate diagrams, and our own classification ([ADR 0017](development/decisions/0017-climate-classes-and-normals.md)).
- **`data/` is a general data library** ([ADR 0012](development/decisions/0012-selectable-parameterised-datasets.md), ADR 0014, ADR 0023):
  - **Manifests** choose which data, and how much, from fixed files, TAP queries, ERDDAP, THREDDS (NCSS) and OPeNDAP subsets, or web requests.
  - **Products** are of twenty-one kinds, listed in an index that applications read.
  - **Data requested on demand** go through each application's server.
- **The Earth Atlas as a forecasting system** ([ADR 0031](development/decisions/0031-observations-scores-and-hazards.md)): observations (METAR, NDBC buoys, the user's sensors through `observation/sensors`), forecasts kept and scored on a schedule (`data/acquisition/schedule.py`, `applications/earth-atlas/schedule.json`), ECMWF's open forecasts (IFS, AIFS, ENS probabilities), hazard plug-ins (fire weather, heat, heavy rain, tsunami travel times, aftershocks, volcanic ash), people and regions with exposure, drainage, Sentinel-2 NDVI, and assimilation of real observations. The research arc (inference foundation, C++ toolchain, a primitive-equation model with a real assimilation cycle) is planned in `development/ideas/2026-10-23-the-research-arc.md` and deferred.
- **The generation of spacetime by field theories** ([ADR 0040](development/decisions/0040-the-generation-of-spacetime.md), physis decision 0006): one framework for every theory whose expansion generates discrete spacetimes, in five stages (the action; Feynman graphs as coloured graphs with exact series in N; their 2-complexes, pseudomanifolds and spin foams; the large-N or condensed phase; the continuum), for scalar, vector, matrix and tensor field theories and for group field theories (Boulatov, Ooguri, quartic melonic). The matrix model recovers 2D quantum gravity's γ = −1/2, the tensor models the branched polymers' γ = 1/2; spin-foam amplitudes sum to Turaev–Viro; GFT condensates give Friedmann's equation with a bounce at ρ_c; random quadrangulations and melonic graphs carry spectral and Hausdorff dimensions. The Complex Atlas walks the five stages in its Generation mode and shows spin foams vertex by vertex (boundary spin network, quantum tetrahedron, 6j against Ponzano–Regge).
- **Complexes, symmetry and fields** ([ADR 0039](development/decisions/0039-complexes-symmetry-and-fields.md)): morphe's first slice (85 checks): exact integers, the Smith normal form and homology with generators, presented and finite groups, and the topology of CW and simplicial complexes, π₁, surfaces, manifolds, collapses, dual 2-complexes and coloured graphs; calx's forward differentiation; physis's symmetry (Lie groups, exact and quantum recoupling), geometry (curvature of any metric), spacetime (exact solutions, Regge geometry, causal sets), field theory (lattice fields, Feynman graphs), gauge (lattice gauge theory on any 2-complex, exact two-dimensional Yang–Mills) and quantum gravity (Turaev–Viro, BF amplitudes, coloured tensor models, matrix models), 202 checks; algebraic geometry is planned (morphe decision 0004).
- **The first C++** ([ADR 0035](development/decisions/0035-the-mathematical-foundations.md), [ADR 0036](development/decisions/0036-spiking-networks.md)): calx's first numerical modules (61 checks), physis's units, dynamics and quantum theory of the open qubit (20), and the neuroscience domain's networks of spiking neurons, `snn` (112): twenty-two neuron models as hybrid systems, among them the classical models of excitability, each checked against the bifurcation of its class, and a quantum emitter followed along exact quantum trajectories, plasticity, a parallel engine giving the same spikes on any number of threads, JSON specifications and a command-line simulator, checked against closed forms, firing patterns and mean-field theory; Brunel's network of 12,500 neurons runs a second in 2.6 s on eight threads. The mathematics of the foundations is mapped in [docs/mathematics.md](docs/mathematics.md); further spiking models, quantum ones among them, are planned from a verified survey.

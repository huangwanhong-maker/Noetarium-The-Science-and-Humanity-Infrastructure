Minutes of the reading-room committee
(an invented record, written for the Archive Studio's example; no such committee met)

Present: M. Ferrand (chair), J. Okafor, L. Brandt.

1. The new catalogue. Okafor reported that the card catalogue is now complete for
   the letters of 1890 to 1910, and that the photographs are still uncatalogued.

2. Lighting. Brandt asked that the lamps by the north windows be replaced before
   winter, since readers of the maps complain of the glare in the afternoon.

3. Access. The committee agreed that the parish registers may be read by appointment
   only, and that the oral histories recorded last spring be heard in the listening room.

The chair closed the meeting at half past five.

MERIDIAN ANALYTICS, INC. — OFFICE OF THE GENERAL COUNSEL
(an invented document, written for the example)

14 September 2026

To: N. Okafor, Data Protection Officer, Halden Care Limited

Dear Ms Okafor,

You asked three questions for your annual review.

First, Meridian is not on the Data Privacy Framework List. We began the
self-certification in June and expect to complete it next year.

Second, Meridian has received no request, order or demand from any public
authority for customer data in the last five years. We publish this figure
each January and will tell you at once if it changes.

Third, you asked whether Meridian could attribute Halden's records to
individuals. Our analytics division holds no data about your patients beyond
what you send. I should add, for completeness, that our advertising division
holds device identifiers for a consumer product of ours, and that the two
divisions' systems are separate.

Yours sincerely,

R. Castellan
General Counsel

DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT — EXTRACT
(an invented document, written for the example; no such companies exist)

between Halden Care Limited, of Dublin, Ireland ("the Controller")
and Meridian Analytics, Inc., of Wilmington, Delaware, United States ("the Processor")

Dated 2 March 2026

1. Subject matter

1.1 The Controller runs a patient-support programme for people in Ireland who
    live with chronic respiratory illness. The Processor will analyse usage
    records of the programme's inhaler sensors and report on adherence.

1.2 Each night the Controller will make that day's usage records available to
    the Processor through the Processor's secure upload service, which is
    hosted in the United States.

4. Pseudonymisation

4.1 Before upload the Controller will replace each patient's name, address and
    health-service number with a random study code. The table that links study
    codes to patients is kept by the Controller in Dublin and will not be
    disclosed to the Processor.

4.2 The Processor will receive, for each record: the study code, the year of
    birth, the county of residence, the device serial number, and the time and
    dose of each use.

9. International transfers

9.1 The Parties have entered into the standard contractual clauses set out in
    the Annex to Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, Module Two (controller to
    processor), signed on 2 March 2026 and attached as Schedule 3.

9.2 The Processor is not at present certified under the EU-US Data Privacy
    Framework.

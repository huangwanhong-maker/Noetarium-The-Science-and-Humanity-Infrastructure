COMPLAINT UNDER ARTICLE 77 OF THE GENERAL DATA PROTECTION REGULATION
(an invented document, written for the example)

To the Data Protection Commission
From: C. Whelan
Date: 2 October 2026

1. I am enrolled in Halden Care's patient-support programme. Every use of my
   inhaler is recorded and, as I have learnt from Halden's privacy notice, sent
   each night to a company in the United States called Meridian Analytics.

2. Halden says the records are "coded". They still carry my year of birth, my
   county, the serial number of my device and the minute of every dose. I live
   in a county with few people of my age.

3. Meridian also runs advertising products and holds device identifiers that
   can be matched to the coded records. It can therefore work out who I am.

4. Meridian is not on the Data Privacy Framework List. Halden relies on
   standard contractual clauses. The pseudonyms do nothing against an order
   served on Meridian, which reads the records in the clear.

5. I ask the Commission to order Halden to stop the transfers.

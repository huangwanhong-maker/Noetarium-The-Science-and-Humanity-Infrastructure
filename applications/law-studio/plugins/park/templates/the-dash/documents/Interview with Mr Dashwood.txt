Interview with Mr P. Dashwood, 4 May 2026 (invented)

I was at the café by the north gate when a woman shouted that my son had fallen from the climbing frame. I took my bicycle and rode straight across the grass and along the path to the play area. It took perhaps a minute. People stepped aside. I had my telephone with me. When I got there he was sitting up, his arm hurt; we went to the hospital and it was broken.

To the Clerk of the Council

Sir,

I have your notice of 6 May. I do not deny that I rode my bicycle through the
park on the morning of Sunday 3 May, from the east gate to the bandstand. I have
ridden that way for years.

But the notice calls it "a motor vehicle", and that is wrong. My bicycle has no
motor. It is an ordinary bicycle with seven gears, which I bought second-hand
in 2019. The warden heard the freewheel and nothing else.

A bicycle is not a vehicle of the kind the bylaw is about. The bylaw was made to
keep cars and motorcycles off the paths, as everyone in Avalon knows.

Yours faithfully,

T. Rider

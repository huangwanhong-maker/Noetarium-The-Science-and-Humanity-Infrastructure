PARK WARDEN'S LOG — BOROUGH OF AVALON (an invented document, written for the example)

Sunday 3 May 2026

08.40  Gates opened. Lawns wet. Nothing to report.

09.10  A man rode in at the east gate on a bicycle and went along the lime walk
       past the fountain at some speed. I called to him to stop. He did not.
       I took it for an electric bicycle from the hum and from how fast it went
       up the rise, though I did not see a battery.

09.25  Spoke to the same man at the bandstand. He gave his name as Mr T. Rider of
       4 Mill Lane. He said he was late for work and that nobody had told him
       bicycles were not allowed. Nobody was hurt and there was no emergency
       of any kind that I could see.

09.40  Reported to the council office by telephone.

16.00  Gates closed.

                                                        J. Okoro, warden

NOTE OF A STILL FROM THE EAST GATE CAMERA (an invented document, written for the example)

Frame taken 3 May 2026, 09.09.52.

The still shows a man on a bicycle passing the east gate towards the lime walk.
A dark box is fixed to the down tube of the frame. In the opinion of the
officer who made this note the box is a battery, and the bicycle is
motor-assisted.

Note made 5 May 2026 by the council's enforcement office from the still as
received.
